## 6. Advisor insight: her own words on the two surfaces she sees every day

The phone file is, by this track's own design, mostly room-language captions with her script on cards she sees for a few seconds each. But there are two surfaces on her phone she will see every single day for years: the lock-screen still and, after the reveal, the file's name in the gallery. The track already restricts the lock screen to "her script end-card line" and the file name to "the title in her language", and W-35 already says her handwriting is used only for words she herself wrote. Take that one step further: make both of those her *own* words, not a verse the team chose. The line on the lock screen should be something she actually says, the blessing or the phrase the maker has heard from her a thousand times, in Gujarati, in her hand if a sample exists (W-35), checked by a Gujarati reader for spelling and shaped through the real pipeline (Exercise 7). The file name after the reveal should be the film's title in her script, not a transliteration. Then the phone does what no venue screen can: it greets her every morning with the film in her own voice, and nothing on it could belong to anyone else. The maker can answer this from memory alone (D-03) and nothing in it tips her off (D-02).
