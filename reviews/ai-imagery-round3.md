## 8. Advisor insight

The pipeline already produces, for every hero photo, an L0 scan and an L1 cleaned copy, and principle 9 says to show the untouched original beside the restoration at least once. Do not show them side by side. Show them in time. Let the print arrive on screen as it is in the album today, magenta and flat, and let the per-channel correction happen in front of the audience over about a second and a half, so the fifty years of dye fade lift off while they watch. That is a keyframed dissolve between two files the maker already has (`xfade` between `__L0` and `__L1`, tested in the sandbox), so it invents nothing and needs no GPU. It makes Principle 4 visible without a caption: the audience sees exactly what was changed, because they watched it change. And it gives the film an ending nobody has to write. The last photographs, this year's phone pictures, need no restoration; so the final image can be the first photograph of the film returning to its faded state, as it actually sits in the drawer. The fade is not damage to hide. It is the only thing in the archive that measures the time between then and now, and the film can let it do that work twice: once lifting, once settling back.
