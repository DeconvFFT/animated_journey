## 8. Advisor insight

The playbook now measures steadiness to a hundredth of a pixel and ease to a named curve, and that is right for the camera. But the same discipline, turned on the holds, would give the maker the one number nobody else can give her. Step 7 and T1 MO4 fix minimum holds (0.75 s, 1.0 s, 1.5 s) from a third-party guide and mark them (J). Her photographs have their own hold lengths: the time it takes the maker to find her in a group picture, the time before the kitchen table registers as that kitchen table. When the slice is cut, have the maker watch each held photo once, cold, and tap the moment she has "seen" it; log that time next to the shot. The median of those taps, by shot class, becomes the hold floor for the film, with the strangers' sheet (question H, too long or too short) as the check that a stranger needs about the same time. It costs ten minutes per chapter, it needs no tool, it stays inside the sole keeper, and it replaces the one borrowed number in the motion section with one that is hers.
