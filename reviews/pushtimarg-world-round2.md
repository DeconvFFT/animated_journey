## 7. Advisor insight: the two words she says to the phone

The film must arrive on her Android phone as well as on the venue screen (D-27), and T7 holds one line of sampradaya text that is also ordinary speech: the greeting, "Jai Shri Krishna" (જય શ્રી કૃષ્ણ, [2]). In a Gujarati Pushtimarg family it is how every phone call opens and closes, how a door is answered, how a child is sent off. It is the one card in T7 that needs no printed-source check beyond her own usage (Q5), carries no deity figure, no verse, no clearance, nothing the hagiography rule touches, and only the Gujarati shaping fixture to pass. If Q5 confirms she says it, make it the film's last card, in her script, at the phone floor, and nothing else on that frame. On her phone it is the screen saying what she says when she picks up. In the public room it is the one line that a Vaishnav will answer aloud, which is the sound the reveal wants and the maker cannot script. If Q5 says her family does not say it, the insight is void and nothing else changes.
