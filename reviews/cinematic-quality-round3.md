## 8. Advisor insight

The maker's blind line-up has a by-product the note treats as bookkeeping: step 4 of the protocol records "what feature tipped the choice", and that feature becomes a board attribute. Read it the other way round. Those features are the maker's own unspoken knowledge of her face, written down for the first time: the parting, the way she tilts her head when she listens, the hand she rests her chin on. Make the one or two features that survive all three age boards (6, 25, 55) the film's visual leitmotif. The hand-made material always gets them right, the cut lands on them when the eras change (a match on the same tilt of the head across forty years is the most honest "same person" device there is, and it answers the strangers' question E without a caption), and nothing is invented because the features come from her photographs. It costs nothing in the surprise, it gives the reconstruction marker something to be faithful to, and after the reveal it gives the maker one true sentence to say to her: "this is how I always knew it was you."
