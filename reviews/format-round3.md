## Advisor insight

The surprise forbids her voice in the premiere cut, and Principle 4 forbids a synthetic one. But route A makes the film a function of `timeline.json`, and the archive master is a twenty-year object that she, not the room, will keep. So reserve the last interlude slot in the timeline for a scene that cannot exist until after 2027-02-27: her own voice, recorded on a phone in the first minutes after the lights come up, saying whatever she says. In the week after the premiere the agent regenerates the family cut and the archive master with that clip as the coda (seconds of render, no re-edit), and the copy she keeps becomes the only version whose ending is spoken by its subject. It is real speech, really said, so Principle 4 is honoured rather than strained; it turns the one thing the surprise took away (her voice) into the one thing the premiere cut can never have; and it gives the "default path plays" rule its truest instance: the film ends on its own in the room, and finishes properly only once she has answered it.
