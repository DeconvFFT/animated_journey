## 8. Advisor insight

The ORA contract already gives the pipeline every layer of every still as its own PNG (`doc.exportLayers` in the read-back step), with the real scan on one layer and the drawn or restored work on the others. Charter principle 4 says reconstructions must stay visibly reconstructions; the playbook currently satisfies it with a label in a log. Use the layer stack on screen instead. For each chapter still, let the animation begin on the illustrated scene and, at the moment the memory is told, fade the drawn layers down so the actual print surfaces through it, paper border, cast and all, then let the drawing settle back over it. The real photograph becomes the thing the whole image was resting on, which is literally true of how the file was made, and the audience sees the difference between what was kept and what was imagined without a caption. It costs nothing new: the per-layer PNGs, the stable layer names (`L10_sky`, `L20_figure`) and the border measurement from the pilot are all already in the hand-off, and the maker's touch-ups in PhotoCraft land on exactly the layers that will be seen moving.
