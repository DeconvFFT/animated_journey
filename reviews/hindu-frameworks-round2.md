## 7. Advisor insight: the two objects

The kanthi is the truest "followed her through every home" object this track has found, and it is the one object that can never stand under the screen, because it never leaves her neck. That is not a problem to solve; it is the film's grammar handed to us. Let the kanthi be what opens a chapter (W-33: her fingers on the beads, at the hour she says the line Q33 gives us), and let a plain domestic thing that has also travelled with her (the sibling's velan, masala dabbo or thepla tin, the maker's O-08 call) be what stands in the room (W-32). The room then sees the object it can touch, and she sees, in the hands that open each chapter, the object it cannot. On a phone at arm's length, fingers on beads is the smallest shot in the film and the one most hers.
