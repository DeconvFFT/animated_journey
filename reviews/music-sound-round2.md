## 8. Advisor insight

Record the room before the room hears the film. At the Monday 22 February run, with the calibration reel done and the hall empty, record sixty seconds of the venue's own room tone from the back row (phone on a towel, as Exercise 1). Two uses, neither of which touches anything of hers or needs a clearance. First, it is the measurement the loudness decision has been missing: the hall's noise floor, read against the three masters, tells the maker whether -23, -18 or -16 is right better than any listener's impression. Second, it becomes the AMB bed under the film's last twenty seconds and the END cue. When the picture cuts to black, the sound that remains is the sound of the room the audience is sitting in, so there is no seam between the film's silence and the hall's. The stranger in row seven does not hear the film end; they hear the room return, and they turn to look at her. The maker's hummed tune from round 1 can sit just before it: her song, given back by the child, then the room they are all in. That is the hand-over the ending brief in film-story asks for, built from the one sound the sole keeper can record with nobody in on the secret.
