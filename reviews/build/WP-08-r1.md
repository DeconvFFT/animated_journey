## 2. Tests of my own, designed to break it

1. **Privacy: free (non-allowlisted) caption text.** Encoded a 3 s clip with an SRT whose first cue is two lines of made-up private words and whose second cue is the allowlisted Latin string, then ran `phone_check.py` with `--json`. The words appear in none of: the encode's stdout, the checker's stdout, the JSON report, the sidecar. They appear only in `OUT.mp4.ass` (the caption file the maker keeps beside the phone file, by design). The free lines are counted (`free_text_lines`) and measured for position, size and contrast; the allowlisted line is tested for shaping. **Held.**
2. **Borderline position.** A copy of `profile.json` with the phone style's `MarginV` at 30 (box bottom about 88.9%) run through `--style phone --scenario fill_20x9`: **exit 1, fill_20x9 FAIL**, as it must. Side observation: the Devanagari shaping IoU read 0.908 at MarginV 30 against 0.928 at MarginV 40: the IoU moves by about 0.02 with the sub-pixel phase of the line, so the 0.9 limit has a thin margin on this face (nudge 4).
3. **An SRT cue with timing but no text.** `--dry-run` exits 0; a real encode ends in an unhandled `IndexError` in `build_ass` (traceback with absolute paths, exit 1, which collides with "made but a check failed"). Not a wrong measurement, a missing input check (nudge 2).
4. **Output folder that does not exist.** `encode_phone.py tiny.mp4 caps.srt /no-such-folder/out.mp4` encodes video and sound for the whole clip and then dies in `shutil.copyfile` with a traceback. On a 12-minute film that is ten wasted minutes and a traceback for a learner (nudge 1).
5. **Allowlist lint on the real tree:** 15 files, 0 findings; the four planted offences and the positive control behave as the report says (run inside `--quick`).
