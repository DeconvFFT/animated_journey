One bracket after the first mention, marked as the usual form and UNVERIFIED, lets her match the number without working it out; it also helps the Sunday read if she pastes the display form.

Both are right (16 for cards 1 and 2, 30 for the furthest pair of the six tints), but a reader of both will pause; one clause in the README ("16 for this pair, up to about 30 for any two") settles it.
