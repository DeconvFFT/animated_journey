When a relative says "I don't remember which year" or "there was no photo of that", record the gap itself, with the date and who said it, instead of filling it. Those recorded gaps are exactly where the film should become visible reconstruction (charter principle 4): a chapter that shows its seams where memory thins is more moving, and more hers, than one that pretends to a photograph that never existed.
