## 3. The failed criterion: my reading, for the orchestrator

I reproduced the diagnostic from the envelope alone. A 50/50 mix of (201, 70, 60) and (40, 91, 200) in linear light (sRGB transfer) is (149.5, 81.3, 151.4); FilmCraft rendered (149, 81, 151) on both versions, within 0.5 of a level on each channel. Converting the red-channel ramp to linear light gives 0.078, 0.247, 0.333, 0.504, 0.586, 0.750, 0.834, 1.0, so the dissolve is half-way (0.504) at the cut frame. Two things the report does not quite say:

1. **The criterion as written cannot be passed by any gamma-correct compositor.** To hit the encoded average in red, the linear mix would have to be 0.70; to hit it in blue, 0.33. No single frame satisfies both, so the FAIL measures a test-design assumption (an average of encoded values, which is what the stand-in CLI does with Pillow's `Image.blend`, and what ffmpeg `xfade` does), not a FilmCraft defect. R8's intent for this line, "the dissolve is centred and half-way at the cut", is met.
2. **The R8 rule still says FAIL**, and the implementor was right not to re-score: a pass mark is not changed by the person who ran the test. My recommendation to the orchestrator is to **amend `T2.mid_dissolve` to linear light (restating an unpassable test is a correction, not a lowered bar), re-run only `bash spikes/sp1_craft_cli/run_sp1.sh --only filmcraft v0.9.0 --profile lean` (about 5 min, one 70 MB re-fetch with a recorded ledger exception), and add the dissolve-stepping check of nudge 2 at the same time.** Until that happens the outcome as written stands, and it costs nothing: "FilmCraft maker-side, ffmpeg and code in the container" was the plan's default anyway, and nothing in weeks 2 to 4 depends on FilmCraft rendering in the container.

Whichever way the orchestrator rules, the report's practical rule is right and should go into the pipeline playbook: **one chapter, one dissolve route** (FilmCraft blends in linear light; ffmpeg `xfade` and the Chromium route blend encoded values; a mixed chapter would have two different-looking dissolves).

## 5. Nudges (non-blocking)

1. **Orchestrator, by Fri 23 Oct (the R8 decision date):** rule on `T2.mid_dissolve`: keep the FAIL as written, or amend the mark to linear light and re-run the one 0.9.0 stage. Record the ruling in `spikes/VERDICTS-W2.md` (T2 row and SP1 row) either way, so the P-LOCK reader sees a decision, not an open question. If the mark is amended: change the criterion in `drivers/t2_filmcraft.py` (compute the expected mid-point from the linearised neighbours; keep the 2-level tolerance), add the same case to the stand-in CLI so `fake.filmcraft_stage_and_keep` still proves the path, and re-cut nothing by hand.
2. **Read the dissolve from the export, not only from `render --seconds`.** The single-frame probe shows 8 distinct blend levels over 12 frames (pairs of identical frames, linear steps as large as 0.17). If an exported dissolve steps the same way, a 0.5 s dissolve between flat areas will judder on the venue screen. Cheapest check: in `phase_export`, place one 12-frame cross dissolve inside the 2,400-frame H.264, sample the 25 frames around it with `ffmpeg -ss`, and record the number of distinct levels as a new criterion `T2.dissolve_steps` (mark: at least 11 distinct levels over 12 frames). On the maker's side, `build_guide.py` (which generates `MAKER-CRAFT-ZIPS.md`) should add to the FilmCraft five-minute test: "step through the dissolve one frame at a time (arrow keys); every frame should differ".
3. **Propagate the R8 outcome** (orchestrator; the files are protected or generated): `ROADMAP.md` row "Craft CLIs" ("Only if spike SP1 T2/T4 pass") should now read the outcome per app; `playbooks/agent-pipeline.md` and the `agent-first-production-pipeline` skill (staged copy) should state "FilmCraft maker-side; EffectCraft titles, line-draw and parallax drafts in the container; one dissolve route per chapter"; `build/BUILD-STATUS.md` issues 6 and 13 can close against this review.
4. **Stale-check provenance string.** Reword the mark text in `drivers/t2_filmcraft.py` line 285 so it stops matching `S-01.at-25-fps` (for example "R8 pass mark, scaled to 2,400 frames at this film's 24 fps from R8's 3,000-frame figure"), then re-cut the baseline so the two `VERDICT-SP1A.md` entries drop out again. The orchestrator should confirm the re-cut the implementor made is acceptable (it added two hits and dropped 96 entries, so the gate is tighter, but the header of `stale_check.py` ties re-cuts to register changes).
5. **One writer per verdict file.** The SP1 edit to `VERDICTS-W2.md` was overwritten by the SP3 edit at 01:40 and had to be re-applied by script at 02:04. Until the sweep settles, either serialise edits to that file through the orchestrator or have each spike write its row to `spikes/rows/<sp>.md` and let a small script assemble `VERDICTS-W2.md` (then `check_verdicts.py` checks the assembled file).

Let the exception name the actual reason (`kept copy consumed by WP-10 T3` versus `deleted with its tree`), so the ledger stays a true record when it is read at P-LOCK.
