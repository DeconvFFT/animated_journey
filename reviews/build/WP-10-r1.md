2. **`gaps.py` boundaries.** A gap of exactly 30 s is not listed (the rule is "over 30 s"): kept awake, exit 0. 3. **`job.sh` stopped by TERM mid-run** wrote `# end ... reason=term`, exited 0, and `gaps.py` says `killed` (exit 1 in the fixture test `log_term.txt`). **Held.** Bad arguments (`--seconds 0`, an existing log) are refused with exit 2 and nothing written. **Held.**
