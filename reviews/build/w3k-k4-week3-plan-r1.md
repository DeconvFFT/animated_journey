2. **Coverage.** Every item the brief named has a package, owner paths, a wave, acceptance commands and refs. "Nothing else in the plan changes with an answer" is the property that makes the Mon 26 Oct pass a single pass, and I could not find a field that breaks it.
4. **Privacy and the public repo.** The real venue answers go to `venue.local.json` (ignored), the coverage input and strip stay outside the repo, raw pastes stay in chat or scratch and only derived files are filed, audio never enters the repo or chat, no voice is cloned, every new tool refuses a name-shaped id, fixtures are generated at test time and nothing tracked is an image. 5. **Hindu-only rule and real memories.** The plan touches no imagery; the one place it could (the Gujarati slice fixture) is test cards and a tone ladder with nobody in it, and no Gujarati cue reaches a screen before `checked_by_reader` is true (O-62). r10's "no frame interpolation unless `--interpolate`, which marks the output" keeps W-40 and the charter's real-memories rule in a conform tool.

The plan's section 2 row 1, W3-07 (b)(iv) and W3-08's two named hand-overs still schedule that work. The plan should pick one on Mon 26 Oct rather than discover it on Fri 30 Oct.
