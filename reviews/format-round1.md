## Critical issues (must fix before approve)

**C1. The surprise is a known fact, and the fallback depends on an asset the surprise rules out.** CHARTER.md Known facts (2026-10-18): the subject is the maker's mother, "She doesn't know," and nothing may tip her off. Yet `research/format.md` Open questions 5 lists "surprise or co-making" as an unknown that "changes the answer"; Pitfalls ("Dignity and consent") says "Show hard chapters to the subject and relevant family first"; the Plan says "record voices" in weeks 1–3; and F-0 "voice and photos," the named fallback, is defined by "the person's real voice." `playbooks/format.md` Step 7 says "her real voice," and F-0 repeats it. In a surprise, her voice can come only from recordings that already exist (home video, voicemail, voice notes) or from relatives speaking about her. If none exist, the fallback has no spine. The playbook flagged Decision 6 correctly but did not follow the thread to Step 7 and F-0. Fix in nudge 1; the inventory question goes to research follow-up 1.

**C2. The charter's production approach is absent from the format decision.** CHARTER.md Known facts: "Production approach: agents generate, apps refine … code-driven animation … heavy work happens in the cloud wherever possible," and Principle 7, "prefer scriptable tools." The research's Tools table scores Resolve as "GUI-first; fine for a film master," the matrix has no criterion for whether agents can generate or regenerate the piece, the skill's gates (a)–(g) have no scriptability gate, and the playbook's Step 7 says "Which editor you have is still unknown. TODO(research): confirm the installed editor" when the charter names FilmCraft (alpha, with a required fallback) on Windows. This matters to the *format* decision because it is the film's strongest unstated advantage: a linear film assembled from a text timeline plus an asset folder is the one format an agent team can regenerate end to end in the cloud, with the maker's hands in FilmCraft or Resolve only for judgement calls. The explorable formats cannot say that. Fix in nudge 2; the tool question goes to research follow-up 2.

## Advisor insight

The surprise means the one voice missing from a tribute to her is hers. Do not treat that as a hole to paper over with relatives; make it the structure. Build the piece in two cuts that the dual-master plan already pays for. The **premiere cut** (Feb 27) is the question: her life as the people who love her kept it, with a deliberately unfinished last chapter called *Now*. In the weeks after the reveal, when she is no longer a secret from herself, sit with her and record her watching it, chapter by chapter, and let her correct, add and laugh. Her reactions and corrections become the voice track of the **archive cut**: the twenty-year master is the one with her in it, the "you remembered that" moments captured rather than hoped for, the wrong details fixed in her own words, and *Now* finished by her. The sidecar-caption and separate-audio-stem discipline in T4 makes this almost free technically; emotionally it turns the gift from something done *to* her into something finished *with* her, and it is the only version of this piece that nobody else could ever make.
