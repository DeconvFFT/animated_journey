## 7. Advisor insight (what would make the film more hers)

The track's most useful measurement cuts both ways, and the playbook has so far read only one side of it. **A closed-mouth hum is the sound a mother actually makes over a cradle, lips shut, and it vanishes on her phone; an open vowel carries on the phone and is not quite what she did.** The playbook's answer is to use the open take on the phone file. Keep that, but do not let the phone's limit rewrite the theatre's truth: the venue master (2K DCP and the 1080p ProRes, full-range speakers) should carry the **closed-mouth take**, uncleaned, because in a room that can reproduce 300 Hz it is the truer sound, and the phone file carries the open-vowel take because there it is the only sound that survives. Two masters already differ in picture (captions burned in, the phone's safe area); letting them differ in this one cue is the same honesty applied to sound, and both takes are the maker's own voice of the same tune, so no rights row changes. The cue sheet needs one more value for `premiere_cut` on the MOTIF and END rows: `S-HUM-closed (venue) / S-HUM-open (phone)`, and the Android tone ladder decides whether the phone can take the closed one after all. The tune she sang should sound, in the room where she first hears it, the way she sang it.
