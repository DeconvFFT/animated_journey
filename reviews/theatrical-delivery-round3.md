## 8. Advisor insight

Build every removable appearance with its honest understudy, before picture lock. The run sheet now says it plainly: after the Sat 6 Feb lock, a withdrawal up to Fri 19 Feb can only remove, never re-cut. Under only-me mode nobody has been asked yet, so every Tier B face and every Tier C hold in the public cut is a shot that might have to go in the thirteen days when the picture cannot move. Rather than hoping, give each such shot a second rendering now: the same beat told through the object, the place, the letter or the hand-made material that `animated.md` already reserves for what was never photographed. Store both; the rights log's `substitute_asset_id` column already has the slot. Technically it costs little (the substitute is usually a Class A or B shot, and the swap is a single-shot re-export inside TD-L8). But the real gain is in meaning: the understudy is the disclosure convention doing its proper work, so a withdrawal does not leave a hole or a hurried crop, it leaves a frame that honestly says "someone was here and chose not to be shown", in the same material the film uses for everything it could not photograph. A film about a life should be able to lose any one person and still be whole, because that is what the life did. And when her version is made on 24 April with the voices turned on, the understudies become the places where the chorus can speak over an absence rather than a face.
