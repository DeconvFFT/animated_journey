## 3. Critical issues (block approval)

**C1. Her found voice in the public cut contradicts `surprise-ops` Decision 18 and the stranger test.** `research/film-story.md` Q4, Templates item 3 (fallback ladder "A then B then E then F"), Q7 ("the trace": "a few seconds of her found voice"), and `playbooks/film-story.md` Decision 3 and Template 8, and `SKILL.md` step 6 all permit `HER` in the public cut after a 72-hour double decision. `surprise-ops` Decision 18 holds her own recorded voice in the T9 ledger for *her* post-reveal decision and says it "never opens a hard chapter". The 72-hour rule is a hard-chapter guard, not a consent substitute for a living person's private recording played to strangers. Resolve it, and declare the delta either way (see nudge 1).

**C2. Cross-file inconsistency an agent will hit immediately.** `SKILL.md` step 6: "MAK-CARD is allowed for the cold-open contract card only." `research` Q2 and Templates item 2 balance check 4, and `playbooks` Checklist B: MAK-CARD capped at about 10 percent of runtime, with the collector stance built on plain maker cards ("I don't know what she was like at seven. Her sister does."; "No photographs survive from these years"). The skill as written forbids the very cards the structure depends on (nudge 5).

## 7. Advisor insight

Cast the object before the story. The one body of evidence the sole maker can gather today, covertly, without asking anyone, is her present: the kitchen table, the keyring, a shopping list in her handwriting, the sound of her front door, the chair she always takes. Choose the object clock from the present and trace it *backwards* through the albums as they arrive, instead of waiting for childhood material that may never come. Three things follow. The vertical slice can start in week 5 on evidence already in hand, with no relative yet reached. The first thirty seconds and the last thirty seconds become the same object, seen twice (rule R2, planted in an ordinary scene), so the stranger in row seven ends the film knowing one true thing about her that the family has looked past for years. And the film's spine is made of the one thing a surprise cannot spoil: she will walk past it on the morning of the premiere.
