## 8. Advisor insight

Make hands the film's grammar. The cold open already says "one object in use: hands, not face", and the collector stance already says the maker appears as hands and handwriting. Extend it: every chapter opens on the teller's own hands holding the photograph, letter or object they lent, filmed in their own kitchen under the truthful pretext ("I'm filming the album as you tell me about it"). Hands can be filmed in every relative's house without a face release problem and without anyone performing; they carry age, work and tenderness that a stranger reads instantly; they give the public room one consistent rule (whoever's hands hold the picture is telling), so the chapter-by-teller structure needs no caption to be understood; and they make the ending inevitable, because the last hands on screen can be the maker's, setting the object down, and the film never has to show her face at all. The one face the room is looking at is already in the room.
