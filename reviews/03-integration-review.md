## 2. Overwhelm: can one learner with other commitments follow this?

**The hours do not add up, and the plan says so in two different voices.** `PLAN-ON-A-PAGE.md` ("What you do") and `DECISIONS.md` O-01 recommend "three weekday sessions of 30–45 minutes plus one weekend block". With the weekend Craft block the roadmap sizes at 3–4 hours (W12–W15), that is about 6–7 hours a week. The runtime rule needs H ≥ 16 for 12 minutes and, per `production-scale.md` 36 and 461, 9 minutes at 10 hours a week fits only if the slice comes in near Prior speed. At 6–7 hours the honest plan is F-0. The same page then says the maker spends "the hour or two a day of watching with sound on and leaving coloured markers", which is 7–14 hours a week on review alone, while W-42 and ROADMAP §6 say "5–10 minutes and no more". The maker reads only the plan page. Both sentences must change.

**Where process is heavy relative to a 9–12 minute film** (name what to cut, defer or simplify):

1. *The per-session close-out* (W-26, ROADMAP §4.2): clear Recovery folders, recent-file lists, LightCraft caches and XMP sidecars; update manifests and `renders.jsonl`; commit text; run the backup; run `stale_check.py`; dismount the container. Eight manual steps after every sitting is where a tired person slips. The pipeline already has `lj.ps1 doctor` and `lj.ps1 run`; it needs `lj.ps1 close`.
2. *The ledgers.* ROADMAP §5.1 and §6 name `decisions.csv`, `hours.csv`, `status.csv`, `chapters.csv`, `gates.csv`, `media.jsonl`, `steps.csv`, a consent ledger, a corrections ledger, the licence log, the rights log (T8) with a `fonts` sheet, `rights/flags/`, `renders.jsonl`, `markers.csv`, `venue.json`, the inventory sheet, the cue map and `feedback/<shot>.md`. Nowhere does it say which of these the maker ever types into. It should say: three (`hours.csv`, the inventory sheet, markers in the editor), and merge consent, corrections and rights into one log with a `kind` column.
3. *Pass/fail metrics per chapter* (ROADMAP §6): shot-length coefficient of variation ≥ 0.4; three or more move families in any eight moving shots, none above 40%; holds ≥ 35%; ΔE00 within 6/4/3; half- and double-speed, sound-off and picture-off passes before every G4; the DX-gated 6 LU check. These are good instruments and the agents compute them, but as gates they create fix-work for a 3-minute module. Keep hard pass/fail to what a stranger would notice on a big screen (MO8a/MO8b, captions in the box, -1 dBTP, colour-patch, the L-label present, the AI-tells sweep, the strangers' first question); demote the rest to "reported, not gated" until G-DRAFT.
4. *Two stranger screenings*, the first on a 100-second slice in the week before Christmas, the same week the slice is finished and three other decisions are due. Under only-me the maker also has to recruit, redact and host. S2 (locked cut, week 17, with an over-65 and a teenager) earns its cost; S1 can be one or two people, remote, on the redacted proxy, or the cold self-read with Fable's rubric pass.
5. *Six exemplar films in weeks 2–3* (W-60) on top of the week 2–3 exercises. The roadmap curriculum already made the better call: two clips in week 3, three films optional in weeks 10–11. The register should follow the roadmap here, not the other way round.
6. *Jargon.* The roadmap is for agents and says so, but the maker signs every gate in it. Two codes collide: **L1** is both the process lock (Sat 14 Nov) and honesty level L1 "cleaned" (which travels in file names); **S1** is both spike 1 (`ROADMAP.md` §5.2 "spike S1 T2/T4", §6 "Spikes S1–S5") and stranger screening 1 ("S1 sheets", in the same G-SLICE row). A learner and an agent will both misread these.
7. *The plan page stops on Sun 1 Nov.* After that the maker has only the 362-line roadmap. The page needs a standing rule that its "next 14 days" is rewritten every Sunday.

What is *not* over-engineered and should stay: the runtime trigger date, one-key-three-guards, the 48-hour cold reads, the understudies, the three loudness masters (cheap, scripted), the colour-patch test on each ffmpeg build, `stale_check.py`.

## Critical issues

**C1. The recommended weekly rhythm cannot deliver the plan's own default runtime.** `PLAN-ON-A-PAGE.md` "What you do, what the AI team does" and `DECISIONS.md` O-01 recommend "three weekday sessions of 30–45 minutes plus one weekend block" (about 6–7 hours with a 3–4 hour block). The runtime rule in the same page needs 16 hours for 12 minutes and, by `production-scale.md` 36/461, about 10 hours with a fast slice for 9 minutes. At the recommended rhythm the honest plan is F-0 (6 minutes), which the maker is never told. Fix: in both places replace the rhythm with one that sums to the tier it is meant to buy, and add the missing consequence to ROADMAP risk 13 and W-02: "under 8 hours a week logged in week 1: F-0 (6 minutes) becomes the plan on Sun 1 Nov and 9 minutes the stretch."

**C2. The plan page tells the maker to review for "an hour or two a day".** `PLAN-ON-A-PAGE.md` "What you do" contradicts W-42 and ROADMAP §6 ("5–10 minutes and no more, markers only"). Fix the sentence to "five to ten minutes a day of watching with sound on and leaving coloured markers; nothing is approved for 48 hours".

## Advisor insight

The plan's best idea is that it stopped treating only-me as a limitation and made it the film's form: a public cut of kept things and hands, with the voices held for April. Its weakest sentence is the one that tells the maker how many hours a week to give, because that sentence quietly chooses the runtime, and right now it chooses the one the maker was never offered. Fix the hours sentence first; everything else in this review is housekeeping.
