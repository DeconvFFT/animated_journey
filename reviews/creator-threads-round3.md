## 6. Advisor insight

**Let the hold time be data.** The one creator exemplar this track did verify, Kalina's *Everyday*, works because its rhythm is a rule, not a taste: one photo, one sixth of a second, no exceptions. Our archive cannot do that, and the playbook rightly says the rate must be designed. Here is a design that is a rule and carries meaning: hold each photograph on screen for a length set by how long it had been since the previous photograph was taken. Six months since the last one, a short hold; eleven years, a long one. The manifest already has the column (the date and how sure you are), `kenburns_xfade.py` already takes a per-still duration and needs only a list instead of one `--dur` value, and the whole thing is reproducible by an agent from a spreadsheet. What the audience gets is the shape of a life's record without a single caption: the crowded years of young children flicker past, the thin decade when nobody had a camera out opens into a long, quiet hold on one face, and the room feels the silence of those years rather than being told about it. It is "texture over checklist" made mechanical, in the Minecraft sense of a rule that produces meaning instead of decoration; it makes the reconstructions honest by construction, because a paper-wobble reconstruction placed in a gap is visibly standing in for years with no photograph; and it gives my round 2 insight its hinge for free: the single place the film breaks its own timing rule, holding one photograph far longer than its gap earns, is the turning point of her life, and the audience will feel the rule bend before they know why.
