## Advisor insight

Route A makes the film a *function* of the timeline, and that changes what "cleared for public screening" can mean. Give every shot in `timeline.json` the consent status that the `story` track's inventory template already carries (`public`, `family`, `her-only`), and let the agent render three masters from one source of truth: the **premiere cut** for the public room, which omits anything not cleared for strangers; the **family cut** on the USB drives, which adds the chapters that belong to the people in them; and **her cut**, the one she keeps, which holds the hard chapters the proxy judged true but not for a crowd. Principle 5 stops being a question of what to leave out of her gift and becomes a question of which room sees which shot. The maker makes the consent decision once, per shot, in a text column, and never has to re-edit a film to protect anyone. It costs nothing (the regeneration is seconds), it is impossible with any GUI-first workflow, and it is the only way the public premiere and the private truth of a life stop fighting each other.
