## 8. Advisor insight

The model counts the holiday fortnight (W10 and W11, 20 December to 2 January) as zero hours, and for production that is right. But it is also the one fortnight in the whole schedule when the maker is most likely to be physically in her house, at her table, with the people who knew her, and nobody will think it odd that a phone is out. Nothing in the `gather` line of the model can buy what is available then: the room tone of her kitchen at breakfast; the real sound of her laugh across a table; the objects of the object clock photographed in the light they actually live in; the street at dusk; the way she says a particular word. No archive holds these, no AI may invent them (Principle 4), and after the premiere they can never again be gathered without her knowing why. So treat W10 and W11 not as dead time but as the film's only location shoot: write a ten-line "holiday capture list" now (sounds, objects, rooms, light, one or two sayings), keep production hours at zero, and let the front-loaded-gathering lever (pre-holiday share 0.5) be met by this capture rather than by scans. It costs nothing in the model, it is the texture-over-checklist principle made schedulable, and it gives the one thing that will make a stranger in row seven believe the kitchen on screen is a real room: they will hear it before they see it.
