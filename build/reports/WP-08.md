## 2. How to run (from the repository root)

```bash
bash tests/phone/run_tests.sh --quick                 # about 30 s: static checks, allowlist lint and its negative controls, encode plan, refusals, --selftest, the phone style over every script with strings
bash tests/phone/run_tests.sh --all                   # about 6 minutes: adds the ProRes master (WP-04 harness), the encodes, spec_check, colour_patch, sim_share, fill_20x9, the findings, PHONE-ROW.md, public_lint
bash tests/phone/run_tests.sh --all --keep            # the same, and keeps the 120 MB master and the encoded files under renders/wp08/ for the single acceptance commands below
bash tests/phone/run_tests.sh --list

python3 -I tests/phone/make_master.py --out renders/wp08/master10s.mov --srt renders/wp08/caps.srt --seconds 10 --report renders/wp08/scene_report.json     # inside lj_with_disk 150 in the tests; about 30 s
bash pipeline/phone/encode_phone.sh renders/wp08/master10s.mov renders/wp08/caps.srt renders/wp08/phone.mp4 [--layout renders/wp08/scene_report.json] [--profile phone_720_share] [--style venue_fs23]
bash pipeline/phone/sim_share.sh renders/wp08/phone.mp4 renders/wp08/shared.mp4
python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py renders/wp08/phone.mp4 [--scenario fill_20x9 ...] [--aspect 19.5:9] [--json OUT]
python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py --style phone --json phone_style.json --scripts-json scripts_tested.json
python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py --selftest
python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py --finding-fs23 --out build/evidence/wp08/finding_fontsize23.json
python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py --candidates --out build/evidence/wp08/caption_candidates.json
python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py --shaping-paths --out build/evidence/wp08/shaping_paths.json
python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py --write-row pipeline/phone/PHONE-ROW.md
python3 -I tests/phone/lint_strings.py
```

Exit codes follow 4.3: `phone_check.py` 0 no blocking check failed, 1 a blocking, shaping or burn-match check failed, 2 bad usage, 3 environment; `--finding-fs23`, `--candidates` and `--shaping-paths` exit 0 however the numbers come out; `--selftest` exits 1 if a mechanism does not work. `encode_phone.py` 0 done, 1 made but a check failed, 2 usage, 3 environment (guard refused or tripped). Python files need `python3 -I`; `phone_check.py` needs numpy and Pillow built with raqm (the container has Pillow 12.3.0 with raqm 0.10.5).

## 5. Deviations from the plan and from the task text, with the reason

1. **Level 4.1, not 4.0.** The task text says "H.264 High 4.0"; the build plan (WP-08 item 1), `DECISIONS.md` W-63 and the card all say High 4.1, and the register wins. `profile.json` carries 4.1 and `spec_check.py` accepts level 41 only. At 1080p24 the two levels carry the same picture; the difference is only the level number in the stream.
2. **32 characters a line, not 36.** W-18 and `mobile-phone-delivery` say at most 36; the plan and the task say 32. The profile carries 32 (stricter, inside 36) and PHONE-ROW names the difference. The orchestrator picks one. At FontSize 28 a 29-character Latin line is about 50% of the picture width and the widest two-line Devanagari cue about 48% (both inside the 90% box). For Indic scripts the character count is only a guide (W-18): the box check is the rule.
3. **Outline 2 (7.5 px at 1080p).** The caption outline is the playbook's and the skill's value (`Outline 2` on the 384x288 canvas), not a number I chose; an earlier draft of `profile.json` carried 0.8 without a measurement and I replaced it.
4. **PH2 cap 3.5 Mbps.** The plan says about 3.5, W-63 says 4. 3.5 is inside both; the measured PH2 file is 0.42 Mbps on the synthetic card.
5. **The 1-px hairline rule has two readings.** `thresholds.json` gates a line of 3 px or more and only reports thinner ones (W-38's floor), so a real file with a 1 px line reports it without failing. The self-test asserts the plan's sentence ("a 1-px hairline frame fails `shared`") by declaring the line meaning-bearing (`gate_from` 1): the 1 px line reaches 0.44 px on the 480p grid and keeps 0.26 of its contrast and fails; the 4 px control keeps its contrast and passes. Both readings are in the evidence.
6. **Mark zones.** The plan says each mark's zone comes from differential rendering with and without its codepoints. A virama that only joins two consonants adds no ink of its own, so its zone is the full string's ink inside the columns where the two renderings disagree (basis `window_ink` in the evidence); marks that add ink use the added ink (basis `added`). A mark survives a scenario when the burned ink has at least 2 px in the zone after the scaling to the scenario's grid and is closer to the reference with the mark than to the reference without it (IoU guard 0.5, the plan's "another letter's ink must not count").
7. **IoU after registration.** The libass drawing is registered on the Pillow-raqm reference by a translation (1/8 px) and a small uniform scale, because libass maps FontSize to an em by its own rule and quantises advances (an unscaled comparison read 0.62 to 0.67 for plain Latin text that looks identical). The IoU the plan asks for is therefore a shaping test (same glyphs, same arrangement), not a size test.
8. **Latin x-height from the font file.** For Latin the x-height and cap height are the font's own OS/2 values times the em fitted to the libass ink (a parse of the WOFF in the standard library); the ink height of the caption (50 px for the test string) agrees with the cap height. For other scripts the base-letter band (ink height with the marks removed) stands in, as `thresholds.json` says.
9. **The allowlist lint.** "No other Latin or Indic test string" is implemented as: every Indic word in any text file under `pipeline/phone`, `tests/phone` and `skills-staging/phone-delivery` is an allowlisted word; every caption fixture line is allowlisted strings joined by single spaces; no Latin phrase of the allowlist's own family ("Test" plus digits) other than the allowlisted one; a script with strings and no `marks[]` fails (Latin has no marks and is exempt). A Latin sentence in prose or code cannot be told from an identifier, so that case is not claimed.
10. **File checks need a caption-free frame.** The differential method finds the burned captions by differencing a cue frame against a caption-free frame of the same file and assumes a white fill over a picture that is static under the captions (the test master is). `--no-diff` uses the libass drawing of the burned captions instead; a real film's free text is measured for position, size and contrast only and its marks are NOT TESTED.
11. **Guard use.** `lj_with_disk` refuses nesting, so the master render (150 MB) and each encode (100 MB) are separate guarded steps; the test run itself is not one stage. `--all` removes the 120 MB master at the end unless `--keep` is given (the plan's persistent figure for WP-08 is 0).
12. **Re-running the plan's commands.** `encode_phone.py` and `sim_share.sh` replace an existing output only when its sidecar says they made it, so the plan's exact commands can be run twice; any other existing file is refused (exit 2) unless `--overwrite`.
13. **Added beyond the plan**, inside owner paths: `--candidates`, `--shaping-paths`, `--style`, `--write-row`, `tests/phone/lint_strings.py`, `tests/phone/make_master.py`, `phone_720_share` checks, the 50 cm INFO line (the playbook's Exercise 4 reads at 50 cm), the title and headline stroke checks, `thresholds.json` keys `file_check` and `distance_cm.proxy_cm`.
14. **No PowerShell.** Every WP-08 tool is Python (standard library, plus numpy and Pillow for `phone_check.py`) or bash, so the Windows-script rules of 4.7 (5.1 and 7 parsing) do not apply; the Windows behaviour of the Python tools is UNVERIFIED (section 4).


## 6. Findings

* **F1. FontSize=23 on a phone, measured (not asserted).** In Noto Sans (the lock file's face) the venue caption's Latin x-height is **2.82% of the picture height** (30.4 px at 1080p), under the 3.2% floor in every view. Sideways it reads **17.99 arcmin at 35 cm** against a floor of 18: a tie within the measurement, so the arcminute verdict is not the one to rely on; the percentage verdict is not close. Its box ends at **91.8% (Latin) and 92.6% (Devanagari)** of the picture height against the 88% limit of `fill_20x9`, so a filled 20:9 screen cuts off up to 12% of the ink. Same FontSize, different face, different size: the research fixtures used Liberation Sans (3.81% at FontSize 23); Noto Sans gives 2.82%. The finding is in `finding_fontsize23.json`, per scenario, with the phone style beside it.
* **F2. FontSize 28 reaches the floor and the 35 cm target and misses the 3.8% target.** Latin x-height 3.44% (37.1 px), Devanagari base-letter band 3.38% (36.5 px). Sideways: 21.96 arcmin at 35 cm (target 21), 19.22 at 40 cm, **15.37 at 50 cm (below the floor of 18)**; filled to the screen 29.5, 25.8 and 20.6. The candidate table (`caption_candidates.json`): FontSize 24 reads 2.94% and 26 reads 3.17% (both under 3.2%), 28 is the smallest that meets the rule, 30 (3.67%, 20.5 arcmin at 40 cm) and 32 (3.92%) also meet it and move the block up (top 67.3% and 66.1%). The rule picks 28, which is also W-18's FontSize. The 50 cm line is why question 3 of the phone test exists: Exercise 4 of the playbook asks for legibility at 50 cm, and the model says sideways-fit letters are marginal there.
* **F3. The fill crop.** The phone caption block spans 68.2% to 85.4%; the limit is 88% (the 90% crop line minus 2 points). The measurement comes from the encoded test file itself, found by differencing a cue frame against a caption-free frame, and the burned captions match the libass drawing of the caption file (IoU 0.95). A caption burned at the venue's 5% margin ends near 95% and keeps about 11% of its ink (self-test); at the venue's `MarginV` 20 it ends at 92% and keeps about 88%. The plan's phrase "loses its lower half" fits the 5% margin, not `MarginV` 20.
* **F4. `ass` against `subtitles` on ffmpeg 6.1.1 (`shaping_paths.json`).** Devanagari: `ass` **0.928**, `subtitles` **0.359**, `ass` forced to simple shaping **0.359**: the `subtitles` filter on this build is simple shaping, as W-75 says. Latin: 0.950, 0.863, 0.863. The libass log line reads `HarfBuzz-ng 8.3.0 (COMPLEX)` in all three, so **the `Shaper:` line is not proof**, which W-75 also says. One path (`ass`) serves every script and every build.
* **F5. Thin lines.** In the test master a 1 px line keeps 0.29 of its contrast through the messenger model and reaches 0.44 px on the 480p grid, a 2 px line keeps 0.49; 3 px lines keep all of it. That matches W-63's "a 1 px line keeps 0.25 through the smaller proxy" and the 4 px floor of W-38.
* **F6. Marks.** The five Devanagari strings that carry marks give 25 mark checks over the five scenarios (five a scenario): 24 pass, in the sideways, shared and filled views all of them. The one failure is in the advisory `small_portrait` view: the virama that joins two consonants in the second string is an 18-pixel zone at 1080p and leaves no ink on that view's grid (0.375 of 1080p). A virama adds no ink of its own, so its zone is the ligature body (`window_ink`) and the guard is the comparison with the unjoined version. At FontSize 23 the same checks lose three (`finding_fontsize23.json`: portrait one, small_portrait two).
* **F7. Upright is degraded by design.** Portrait reads 12.3 arcmin and the small phone 12.9 at 35 cm, below the floor of 18 for any caption that fits the 90% box; both views are advisory and listed, never gated, and the hand-over text is "turn it sideways" (W-18).
* **F8. Libass maps FontSize to an em by its own rule** (`FontSize x 3.75 x unitsPerEm / (winAscent + winDescent)`: 28 gives 69.1 px in Noto Sans, 55 px in Noto Sans Devanagari at the same FontSize) and quantises advances; an unscaled comparison with Pillow-raqm read 0.62 to 0.67 for plain Latin text, a registration with a small scale fit reads 0.95. The same FontSize is therefore not the same letter height in two scripts, which is what W-18 says about Gujarati and KA.
* **F9. Disk.** The volume ran between 527 and 697 MB free during the work (other jobs write to it). The master render planned 150 MB, wrote about 123, and the guard accepted it with 524 MB to spare; a rerun with less than about 550 MB free is refused by the guard (BLOCKED, never a quiet skip).
* **F10. The test card is rendered at 8 fps by the harness** (10 s in about 30 s with the encode); the sound is a tone ladder, so the two-pass loudnorm numbers say nothing about speech.
