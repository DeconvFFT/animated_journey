## 3. The failed criterion, and what I think it means (diagnostic, not a re-score)

`T2.mid_dissolve` samples the 21 frames around a 12-frame cross dissolve and takes the frame nearest the half-way mix. The test expects the **average of the encoded sRGB values** of the two flat centres, (120.5, 80.5, 130). FilmCraft gives (149, 81, 151) on both versions. A 50/50 mix in **linear light** (sRGB transfer) of the same two colours is (149.5, 81.3, 151.4), within 0.5 of the measurement, so FilmCraft is gamma-correct where the criterion was not. Converting every sampled frame back to linear light gives a clean linear ramp of 12 steps (alpha 0.078, 0.247, 0.333, 0.504, 0.586, 0.75, 0.834, 1.0 at offsets -5, -3, -2, 0, 1, 3, 4, 6; the 12 steps of 1/12 are 0.083 apart) centred on the cut. So the dissolve starts and ends in the right place and is half-way at the cut; only the colour space of the blend differs from what the test assumed. Two things follow if the orchestrator accepts this reading:
* The criterion could be restated in linear light. That is a change to a pass mark after seeing the result, so it is Fable's and the orchestrator's call, not mine. If they amend it, the re-run is `bash spikes/sp1_craft_cli/run_sp1.sh --only filmcraft v0.9.0 --profile lean` (about 5 minutes and a 70 MB re-fetch with a recorded ledger exception).
* Whatever the rule says, a dissolve made in FilmCraft will not look like an ffmpeg `xfade` between the same two clips (ffmpeg blends the encoded values), so one chapter should use one route for its dissolves.

Second observation, not analysed further: the single-frame render probe returned repeated blend levels for pairs of consecutive frames (offsets -5 and -4, -2 and -1, 1 and 2, 4 and 5 are identical), i.e. 8 distinct levels over the 12-frame dissolve. T2's export test counted frames (2,400 of 2,400) but did not read dissolve levels from the export, so whether an exported dissolve steps the same way is UNVERIFIED.
