## 4. What the real runs found (these change how the pipeline uses the apps)

**FilmCraft 0.9.0 (T3, `t3_filmcraft_v0.9.0.json`).** R8 expectations all met. Plain OTIO: 4 clips of 120 frames, 3 cross dissolves of 24 frames centred on the cuts, 2 markers, 1 audio clip, 1920x1080 at 24/1, **no keyframes, Scale 100** (a still imports at native pixels, craft-motion item 6); with `metadata.filmcraft`: 2 keyframes on position and on scale of each clip, values as emitted. Relative media resolve from the `.otio`'s own folder whatever the CLI's working folder is; a lone copy reports its 5 media offline. The whole T3 takes about 10 s and peaks at 100 MB resident.
1. **FCP7 XML and FCPXML hold ABSOLUTE `file://` media URLs** (the exporting machine's folder names; on the maker's PC that is the profile folder). Never commit or paste them. OTIO exported beside `media/` is relative (`media/x.png`); exported into a sub-folder it writes `../media/x.png` (WP-06's validator refuses `..` by design); the EDL's `* SOURCE FILE` lines are relative with `../`. `research/craft-motion.md` F3 says only OTIO is relative: that is right, and it is the only portable one.
2. **The ease is lost**: EaseOut comes back as Bezier through FCP7 XML ("handles not preserved", ×6) and as Linear through FCPXML. Keyframe counts, times and values survive both. The OTIO round trip is identical in every keyframe field.
3. A clip marker written to FCPXML re-imports as a sequence marker. The EDL re-imports with cut frames shifted by the dissolve (0-108, 108-228, 228-348, 348-480) and no motion.
4. Stills carry a 29.97 rate in both XML formats (`30 NTSC`, `1001/30000s`) while the sequence stays 24: the maker's Resolve check (F2) must look at the stills' durations.
5. A bare FCPXML `<video>` is skipped with the documented warning "FCPXML <video> elements are not supported and were skipped".

**PhotoCraft 0.10.0.** `info <ora> --compact` lists the layers top-first with the same names as `stack.xml` (918 commands in the registry); ORA to PNG (size equals the canvas; mean difference from the ORA's own merged image 0.01 and 3.68 levels), ORA to ORA, ORA to PSD and PSD back to ORA all keep the names; **the ORA PhotoCraft writes passes WP-06's `validate_ora.py` (22 checks, 0 FAIL)**. CLI 67.6 MB, peak 33 MB resident.

**VectorCraft 0.13.1.** All 17 (rig) and 11 (`at-rig.svg`) authored ids survive; the `feColorMatrix` filter is named as ignored exactly as the playbook predicted (`filter 'tint' (feColorMatrix) on 'faded' isn't supported; drawn without it`); the viewBox origin is reset to `0 0`; `<style>` and classes are dropped; the PNG at scale 2 is 800x600 and the PDF is valid. **But rect, circle and ellipse elements become paths** (9 of 17 rig elements, 5 of 11 in `at-rig.svg`), so a script that animates `cx`, `cy`, `r`, `x`, `y`, `width` or `height` finds nothing: animate transforms by id. And the `data-*` of the rig's `<text>` element changed (the rig puts them there on purpose, to probe; the playbook rule is "not on text"), so `check_ids.py` exits 1 on the rig. The raw `check_ids.py` exits 0 on `at-rig.svg` (the playbook's own fixture). My first-version driver recorded which element changed but not which attribute; the next run records a per-element diff. CLI 99.2 MB, peak 104 MB.

**LightCraft 0.9.1.** `LIGHTCRAFT_GPU=0` render gives a 2048x1365 JPEG, brighter (mean luma 110.9 to 122.2); the `.lcpreset` import keeps the id `lj-faded-print-a`; importing it a second time is skipped (`skipped: 1`); the reused steps file returns 6 result lines, every `ok: true`; the TIFF export is 16-bit sRGB. **`library.import` skips content duplicates** (3 files scanned, 1 imported, 2 duplicates `reason: content`): two identical scans become one. CLI 103.2 MB, peak 210 MB.

All four CLIs started with no GPU, no missing library and no network (`unshare -rn`).

## 7. Deviations from the plan, with reasons

1. **Stage order**: FilmCraft first, then PhotoCraft, VectorCraft, LightCraft (the plan lists FilmCraft last). Reason: its stage deletes the kept tarball and gives the next three 67 MB more room.
2. **Planned MB computed, not the plan table's 300/450/350/300** (4.9 says "the real figure is computed from the tarball listing"). Before the download only the HEAD size is known, so the CLI is assumed 1.15x the tarball and the exact figure is re-checked against live Avail after the listing (BLOCKED, tarball deleted, if it would not fit). The real runs came in at about half the table's figures.
3. **Only the one CLI member is extracted** (plan: "only the members it needs"), and the tarball is deleted as soon as it is out, to keep the footprint down.
4. **A stage that already ran is not run again** by `--all`, so the acceptance command is re-runnable without a second fetch; `--again` runs it and records the ledger exception.
5. **LightCraft's stage ended FAIL on a flaw of my test content** (three identical scans). Fixed in the driver and in the stand-in; **not re-run on the real tarball**, to keep the exactly-once rule. If the orchestrator wants the proof, it is one recorded re-fetch: `bash spikes/sp1b_readback/run_sp1b.sh --only lightcraft --again`.
6. **Bookkeeping bugs in the first version of the harness, found by the mechanism tests and fixed, whose effect remains in the real envelopes**: `kept_tarball_deleted` is `true` in the PhotoCraft, VectorCraft and LightCraft stage envelopes (meaningless there; it is now `null`), and `fetched` for a refused stage was derived from the plan instead of the ledger (now from the ledger count). I did not edit the produced evidence; `build/evidence/wp10/README.md` says so.
7. **"df within 10 MB of its start"** cannot be asserted literally on a volume other jobs write to; the per-stage deltas and the empty folders are given instead (table, row 7b).
8. **`check_ledger.py` reads the two ledgers, the stage envelopes and the guard's ledger**, not "run logs": the harness keeps no per-stage console log (the acceptance transcript has the console output).
9. **Development use of the kept tarball**: to develop and debug the T3 driver I extracted the FilmCraft CLI from the kept tarball once into the scratch folder (60 MB, under the 100 MB threshold, so outside the guard; SHA-256 re-checked first), ran it by hand, and deleted it again before the real stage. It is not a fetch.
10. **Not done, by the task**: SP3 run, SP4 execution and parts (d) (e) (f), T5, T6; no skill or playbook edited (they are outside my paths; see section 8).
11. **Extra files not in the plan**: `config.json`, `finalize_stage.py`, `stage_budget.py`, `check_verdicts.py`, `make_evidence.sh`, the stand-in CLIs and the fake release builder.
