`T2.mid_dissolve` is amended to a linear-light mid-point (the 50/50 mix of the two neighbours under the sRGB transfer function, same 2-level tolerance), and `T2.dissolve_steps` is added (at least 11 distinct levels over a 12-frame exported dissolve). `drivers/t2_filmcraft.py` and the stand-in CLI changed together. EffectCraft 0.11.1 T4 stays PASS 9 of 9. So the R8 rule on the amended result lets agents drive both Craft CLIs in the container for drafts, with the OTIO and ffmpeg route kept as the fallback; nothing in the plan depends on it yet. The practical rule is **one chapter, one dissolve route**, and this pass measured it: for the same two colours FilmCraft's half-way frame is (149, 81, 151), ffmpeg `xfade` gives (120, 80, 130) and headless Chromium with a CSS opacity of 0.5 gives (120, 81, 130).

### The numbers behind the ruling (from `build/evidence/wp07/t2_filmcraft_v0.9.0.json`)

* Mid-point: neighbours (201, 70, 60) and (40, 91, 200); linear-light mix (149.5, 81.3, 151.4); the frame nearest the cut (offset +0) is (149, 81, 151), 0.47 levels away; the average of the encoded values (120.5, 80.5, 130.0) would be 28.50 away. The first run measured the same frame (149, 81, 151), so the amendment moves the mark, not the measurement.
* Steps in the export (a 2-second H.264 window around the cut, 48 frames, red channel, centre patch): 19 frames pure first still (199), then 11 blended frames 193, 186, 177, 169, 160, 148, 137, 126, 111, 94, 74, then pure second still (39). The 12-frame window that starts one frame before the first blended frame holds 12 distinct levels (mark: at least 11). So the pairs of identical frames that the single-frame `render --seconds` probe showed on the first run (8 levels over 12 frames) do not appear in the export.
* The other 12 criteria: no crash at N=100 and N=300; RSS slope 25.74 MB per still (mark 40), RSS(300) 5,426 MB; 2,400-frame H.264 in 251 s = **9.56 fps** (mark 5; the first run had 11.68, cause not investigated, see below); ffprobe 2,400 of 2,400 frames at 24/1; decode silent; ProRes HQ 24 of 24 frames; `offlineMedia: []`; Ken Burns worst error 0.63 px.
