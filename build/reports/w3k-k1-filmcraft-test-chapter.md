## 2. Decisions taken inside the package

1. **One builder.** `t3_filmcraft.py` has no builder of its own: its `inputs()` shells out to `pipeline/roundtrip/emit_otio.py` with the example spec. So "reuse the T3 builder" is that emitter, now behind `emit_chapter()` and `--emit-chapter`. The fixture and T3 share it; no OTIO is written by hand.
2. **A second card that differs strongly (`card_base`).** With the default cards the pair differs by at most 16 levels in the background and by one glyph, so a 12-frame dissolve between them changes a neighbouring frame by 0.55 to 0.75 levels of 255 over the whole picture (my model: linear-light blend, FilmCraft blends in linear light per the c1 ruling): the maker could not see a step and "every frame should differ" would be untestable by eye. With a teal second card the same model gives 3.7 to 13.2 levels (first step largest, last smallest). The model is not a FilmCraft result. The alternative, drawing the card elsewhere, would have been a second builder; the optional field is small, tested, documented and leaves every old card unchanged.
3. **10 seconds, two 5-second stills.** "Play 10 seconds" then covers the Ken Burns move and the dissolve (at 5 s). The playbook's "Export 20 seconds" no longer matches; the guide tells her to export all 10 s. A 20-second chapter would have put the dissolve at or after 10 s or needed a 15-second hold and a changed `still_available_frames` (the value FilmCraft already read in T3 is 240).
4. **No audio, no WAV.** Keeps the fixture to three files and two small images; SoundCraft is separate. The chapter is silent and the guide says so.
5. **Second card holds still**, so the only move in the chapter is the one Ken Burns move.
6. **Marker `Dissolve starts` at frame 114** (the first blended frame) as a way to find the dissolve; whether FilmCraft shows it in the ruler is UNVERIFIED, so the guide gives the timecodes first and does not rely on the marker.
