Tested: ffmpeg 6.1.1 (Ubuntu 24.04 sandbox), 2026-10-18 [RUN]. Both stock chains below produced the intended
look on a TEST CHART, not on her prints. Not yet run on the maker's PC: TODO(verify on the maker's PC).

=====================================================================
PART 1. THE RULE
=====================================================================
The look is taken from one real album, not from a decade. Pick three prints from that album (after the
pipeline is locked). In each print, sample a known-white object and a skin midtone. Derive the lift, cast
and grain from those samples. Save the result as that album's look, named after her chapter, and apply the
same string to every photo from that album. The same string applied twice must give identical output.

=====================================================================
PART 2. STOCK PRACTICE, NOT FOR THE FILM
=====================================================================
These two chains are generic era looks. They are kept only as exercise material. They must not be used in
the film, because they carry no information about her albums.

STOCK PRACTICE, NOT FOR THE FILM. Chain 1: warm, lifted, softly desaturated, grain, vignette.
  Use: ffmpeg -i in.jpg -vf "<chain>" out.jpg
  Chain: curves=preset=lighter,colorbalance=rs=.12:gs=.02:bs=-.15:rm=.08:bm=-.08,eq=saturation=0.85:contrast=0.92,noise=alls=14:allf=t+u,vignette=PI/5

STOCK PRACTICE, NOT FOR THE FILM. Chain 2: black and white, lifted blacks, rolled-off whites, grain.
  Use: ffmpeg -i in.jpg -vf "<chain>" out.jpg
  Chain: format=gray,curves=all='0/0.04 0.5/0.5 1/0.94',noise=alls=18:allf=t+u,vignette=PI/4

STOCK PRACTICE, NOT FOR THE FILM. Any film look you are licensed to use, as a 3D LUT:
  Use: -vf "lut3d=file.cube"
