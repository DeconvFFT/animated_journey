# Agent-First Production Pipeline

## Purpose

Let agents make Life Journey shots from code and text, rendered in the cloud container where possible. The maker refines a few shots by hand in the Craft apps on Windows. Output must be repeatable, licence-clean and private. The Craft apps are free, early-alpha refinement layers, so nothing critical may depend on them.

Human guide: `playbooks/agent-pipeline.md`. The tools that exist and how to run them: `pipeline/README.md`. What is proven and what is still owed: `build/evidence/P-LOCK-readiness-W2.md`. Evidence, measurements (M1 to M21) and open gaps: `research/agent-pipeline.md`. Charter: `CHARTER.md`. Decisions register: `DECISIONS.md`.

**Reconciled with DECISIONS.md (2026-10-18, and again 2026-10-19 after the her-world merge).** The register wins where this skill disagrees (D-21). Frame rate is settled at 24 fps on the Sun 1 Nov settings card (W-14, W-16). Real family material enters only after the P-LOCK process lock, Sat 14 Nov 2026, and covert collection starts Sun 15 Nov (D-13, W-06). The five spikes are SP1 to SP5 (Codes). The brief carries the Hindu-world and phone fields of W-42; the repo is public (D-14, D-26); the primary drawn idiom is the floor-art line at the phone floor (W-72); no deity is drawn, generated or photographed (W-61); the phone files are PH1 and PH2 (W-63). Only-me (D-03): the maker is the proxy and nobody else reviews; hard chapters follow one-key-three-guards (D-04). Family media never leaves the PC and the encrypted drive: `cloud: none` by default, no cloud copy, a second encrypted SSD (D-15, W-24, W-25).

**Built and proven in the container (weeks 1 and 2, 2026-10-19), on synthetic test material only:** the Route A harness `pipeline/bin/render.mjs` (spike SP2 passed); the encode chains in `pipeline/lib/chains.json`; the validators `pipeline/validators/spec_check.py`, `colour_patch.py` and `mo8_gate.py`; the ORA, SVG-id and OTIO emitters and validators in `pipeline/roundtrip/`; the phone encode and legibility check in `pipeline/phone/`; the repo checks `scripts/public_lint.py` and `scripts/stale_check.py`; the disk guard `scripts/lj_guard.sh`; the check runner `scripts/run_week2_checks.sh`. **Not built yet:** `lint_template.py`, `edit_audit.py`, the manifest schema, the timeline emitters and `lj.ps1 run`. **Nothing has run on Windows.** Every Windows behaviour in this skill is UNVERIFIED until the maker's PC has shown it, and a Linux result for a Craft app (SP1) is not evidence for Windows.

## When to use / when not to

Use it for:
- writing or changing a Route A scene, a Route B photo-motion script, a class C hero shot, or a `brief.yaml`;
- building the render harness, validators, manifests, review packs or gate files;
- preparing a hand-off for PhotoCraft, VectorCraft, FilmCraft or EffectCraft, or validating one that comes back.

Do not use it for:
- story gathering, interviews or dignity decisions (use `life-story-interviewing`);
- consent or label decisions on AI-altered photos (use `ai-photo-to-animation-pipeline`);
- FilmCraft or EffectCraft work on its own (use `craft-apps-motion-refinement`), or PhotoCraft and VectorCraft visual work alone (use `craft-apps-visual-refinement`).

## Procedure

1. **Confirm the phase.** Read the charter. Real family material may enter the pipeline only after the P-LOCK process lock (Sat 14 Nov 2026, proven on synthetic material) and the maker's go; covert collection starts Sun 15 Nov (D-13, W-06). Until then, use synthetic or stand-in material only.
2. **Check the project settings** in the repo: 1920 x 1080; one frame rate for the whole film (24 fps, settled; W-14, W-16); Rec. 709 / BT.1886 for video and sRGB for stills; audio 48 kHz, 24-bit. Never change them per shot.
3. **Classify the shot.** Class A is a parameterised photo move (seeded per photo, and capped by gate G4 in step 16), B a designed graphic, C a hero 3D shot, D a title or caption. Route A is a seekable web scene. Route B is a photo-motion chain. `blender` is for class C only. Hero shots are 5% of shots, about five in the film (four at 9 minutes), and the kitchen is the only built set (W-34). `craft` (EffectCraft titles) runs in the container only after spike SP1 passes; on the maker's PC the EffectCraft template route is available once the five-minute test in `research/craft-motion.md` R7 passes.
4. **Check every input** in `manifests/media.jsonl`. If an input's `cloud` is `none`, the agent still writes the brief, the script and the expected `render.json`, commits them and marks the shot `pc-render`; the maker runs the shot's printed command on the PC and pastes the validator output in chat (`lj.ps1 run <shot>` does not exist yet; `lj.ps1` v0 has `doctor` and a dry-run `close` only, see "Tools that exist today"). Only the cloud render is skipped. Raising the level is never the agent's move. A `preview` set may use only proxies (about 768 px JPEG or 480p). Never upload full-size originals or masters, and never send family photos to a hosted renderer. `full` is for non-family material only (D-15, W-24).
5. **Write `brief.yaml`** with: beat, `source_ref`, duration, route, every input with its `cloud` level, palette (six colours or fewer), motion family, seed, audio cues and acceptance checks, plus the W-42 fields: `motif_check` (hindu, Hindu-Gujarati, flagged, excluded, jain_flag, adivasi_flag or political-room, with a source, W-66), `deity_form`, `deity_source`, `respect_check` (R1 to R20, VR-1 to VR-3, PR1 to PR25), `phone_stroke_ok`, `phone_safe`, `hands_box`, `object_box`, `caption_zone`, `place_id`, `political_room` (W-71), `kitchen_hour` (a T3b value, sheet only, never a card, W-62), `hands_making` and `seat_check` (W-67). A `flagged` brief goes to Fable. Ask the maker for gate G0. Do not start motion until G0 is approved.
6. **For a new look, build three style frames first** and ask for gate G1. Record the palette, each font with its licence file, and each texture in `textures.json` with its source URL and date. Fonts are OFL only, with the Indic families of W-21 (Noto Sans Gujarati for captions, Rasa or Baloo Bhai 2 for cards, Noto Serif Gujarati for verse); her script on titles and verse cards is shaped outlines, never live text. G1 locks the style bible's twelve decisions (W-38; decision 11 is `hindu_idiom`, decision 12 is moving footage).
7. **Build a Route A scene.** The page defines `window.seekTo(t)` as a pure function of `t`, which sets the picture and does nothing else. Build the timeline paused (`gsap.timeline({paused:true})`) and set `seekTo = t => { tl.time(t,false); return true; }`. Set `gsap.config({lazy:false})` anyway; lazy rendering did not change the probe frames (M10). Give every rough.js shape a nonzero `seed` derived from its id. Never use `Math.random()`, `Date.now()` or real-time timers. Set `window.ready = true` after fonts and images load. Vendor GSAP, rough.js and three.js into `pipeline/lib`, with their licence files. The primary drawn idiom is the floor-art line of her region (W-72): a dot grid, one closed stroke per dot, `stroke-dashoffset` driven against `pathLength` from `seekTo(t)`, at the phone floor (meaning lines 4 px or more at 1080, decoration 3, dots and repeat units 6, holes 4, no hairlines; W-38 decision 3). No deity is drawn (W-61); a deity appears only as a symbol or a seva object that is not a svarupa.
8. **Render Route A in shards.** Run `node pipeline/bin/render.mjs --dir pipeline --mount /vendor=vendor --page scenes/<scene>/scene.html --out renders/<shot>/frames --seconds N --shard i/n` (24 fps and 1920x1080 are the defaults; `--frames A:B` renders a range). Each shard writes PNG frames through a temp file and rename, and skips frames that already exist, so a job re-run after a reclaim resumes; never pass `--ffmpeg` to a sharded run. A scene must be self-contained: the harness serves only `--dir` and the `--mount` folders on 127.0.0.1, answers a path that climbs out of them with 404, aborts every request that is not local (exit 2, naming the URL), and with `--fail-on-pageerror` stops at the first page error (exit 2, no partial output). Keep each job at 10 minutes or less (`--max-seconds` stops cleanly and prints a resume hint). Encode with a **named chain**, never with a hand-typed line: `--chain h264_venue --ffmpeg out.mp4` pipes the frames straight into ffmpeg (no PNGs on disk), and `--encode --chain NAME` encodes the PNG frames of a sharded run. The chains are in `pipeline/lib/chains.json` (`h264_venue`, `h264_backup_main40`, `h264_online`, `prores422hq_master`, `prores4444_alpha`, `ffv1_lossless`); the order inside them is part of the chain: scale first, then `format=`, then `setparams=colorspace=bt709:color_primaries=bt709:color_trc=bt709:range=tv`. The bare `-pix_fmt` encode gave BT.601 pixels under BT.709 tags on ffmpeg 6.1.1 (error 23 to 25 of 255), and the format-first H.264 order measured 5/255 against the +-3 tolerance (research Q-F1); `colour_patch.py` reproduces both numbers as its negative controls. **Finding F-12 (new, week 1):** the ProRes 422 HQ master chain, the one that matters most (W-15), had never been measured; `colour_patch.py` measured it on ffmpeg 6.1.1: worst error 2/255 (0 with the accurate decode), so it is approved; the three H.264 chains measure 1/255. Run the colour-patch test again on the PC's own ffmpeg (9.0.2 from winget) before any chain is trusted there (UNVERIFIED). For alpha overlays, use `--alpha` and the `prores4444_alpha` chain (scale first, then `format=yuva444p10le`, then `setparams=`). Then run the validators below and delete the PNGs.
9. **Build a Route B shot.** Use a seeded generator (seed from the brief) to pick direction, duration, ease family, hold, drift and wobble for each photo, so no two photos move alike. Conform each still with ImageMagick (auto-orient, fit to the sequence plus headroom, 8-bit, strip the profile; see R1 in `research/craft-motion.md`). Route B stills are prepared at 3840 px on the long edge (W-15); a 1080p sequence's working copy, and the FilmCraft hand-off copy, is derived from it at about 2880 x 1620. Make grain as an 8-frame pre-rendered overlay, loop it on twos and blend it with `blend=all_mode=overlay:all_opacity=0.35`. Do not use ffmpeg's `noise` filter for long renders (M6).
10. **Run the template and pacing checks.** `lint_template.py` should flag more than 60% of tweens sharing one ease string, uniform stagger, one transition type above 70% of cuts, the same move on every photo, or a zoom-in on every photo. `edit_audit.py` should check that holds after a payoff are at least 0.75 s and that shots under 1.5 s are impact frames only. Neither is built: spike SP5 delivered the colour, spec, MO8 and hand-off validators, not these two (they are week 3 to 4 work). Until they exist, do these checks by hand and say so in the review.
11. **Self-check.** Run the validators in the checklist below (`pipeline/validators/spec_check.py`, `colour_patch.py`, `mo8_gate.py` for the media; `pipeline/roundtrip/validate_ora.py`, `svg_rig.py gate` and `validate_otio.py` for the hand-offs; `scripts/public_lint.py` and `scripts/stale_check.py --new-only` for the repo). Allow at most three fix loops. Write any failure into the review file. Stop and report, and switch to the fallback route, if a validator fails twice, if the critic says `revise` three times, or if a Craft app crashes.
12. **Make the G2 review pack.** Include a contact sheet of 8 timed frames, frame strips for key moments, full-resolution crops of faces and type, a preview of 20 MB or less, and `render.json` (inputs, seed, tool versions, command line, hashes). Run a fresh-context critic twice with different frame orders and compare the answers. The critic sees stills only, so never record a smoothness or sound judgment from it. Ask the maker to listen, including on the project Android's own speaker at 50% volume (W-19). Composite `safe_area_template.svg` on three frames per shot and carry the arm-and-attribute count on any deity symbol (W-42, W-63). Fable reads every flagged brief, every deity shot, every verse card, every capture-log entry that names a seva object and every phone surface.
13. **Revise at most twice, then freeze the scene.** Answer each line of the maker's `feedback/<shot>.md` with `done`, `declined (reason)` or `question` in `render.json`.
14. **Hand off to a Craft app** only in these formats:
    - PhotoCraft: OpenRaster `.ora` (a zip of PNG layers plus `stack.xml`). Layer names are the IDs. Use no adjustment layers, layer styles or live text. Layers are 8-bit sRGB straight-alpha PNGs.
    - VectorCraft: SVG with unique IDs matching `^[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9._-]*$`, `data-pivot` and `data-z` on groups and shapes, and `viewBox="0 0 W H"`. Do not rely on `class`, `<style>`, group transforms or SMIL or CSS animation. Run svgo only as a lint, with `cleanupIds:false`, because its default preset strips every ID (M17).
    - EffectCraft: a static SVG subset in, and an RGBA PNG sequence or ProRes 4444 out. RGBA PNG sequence preferred; ProRes 4444 only after the colour-patch test passes, because EffectCraft issue #340 reports ProRes 4444 as "tagged bt709 but sRGB-encoded" (`research/craft-motion.md`). Use `effectcraft-cli render` in the container only after spike SP1 passes; on the maker's PC the template route is available once the five-minute test in `research/craft-motion.md` R7 passes. Source is at `https://github.com/storytold/effectcraft` (tag v0.11.1) [68].
    - FilmCraft: pre-conformed 8-bit sRGB stills; an OTIO timeline with `metadata.filmcraft` for motion; H.264 8-bit 4:2:0 or ProRes at a constant frame rate; relative `target_url` media paths (`media/chNN_sNN.png`) resolved against the OTIO's own folder, never a `file://` URL (Kdenlive does not decode it; see `craft-apps-motion-refinement` step 6). Never hand-write an `.fcproj`.
    - What the Linux command-line runs of the pinned apps showed (WP-10; Linux CLI results, **not evidence for Windows**): PhotoCraft keeps ORA layer names and writes ORA that `validate_ora.py` passes. VectorCraft keeps every authored id but **turns rect, circle and ellipse into paths**, so animate transforms by id and not `cx`, `cy`, `r`, `x`, `y`, `width` or `height`, and it changes the `data-*` of a `<text>` element (keep them off text). FilmCraft takes motion from OTIO only through `metadata.filmcraft`; its FCP7 XML and FCPXML hold **absolute** `file://` media paths (never commit or paste them) and lose the ease on re-import, so OTIO is the only portable hand-off; stills carry a 29.97 rate in both XML formats while the sequence stays 24. Emit and check hand-offs with `pipeline/roundtrip/emit_ora_layers.py`, `emit_otio.py`, `validate_ora.py`, `validate_otio.py` and `svg_roundtrip.sh`.
    - Every export gets a new file name. Never overwrite. Validate it on return.
    - A Craft app touches only a copy in `work\`, a chapter whose spec and OTIO are in the repo, or a title whose SVG fallback exists. It is never the only writer or reader of a format, never the holder of the only copy, never a step in the week before the premiere, and the Launcher is never installed (D-12, W-22).
15. **Hand back and render the final.** Validate the export. Re-attach the GSAP timeline and classes by ID. Render at full resolution and save as `..._v00N_appr`. Add a line to `manifests/renders.jsonl`.
16. **Assemble the chapter.** Emit the neutral timeline spec and generate the OTIO file. Validate it. The maker opens it in FilmCraft on the PC and exports ProRes 422 HQ. Make the H.264 access copy with ffmpeg, then measure loudness with `ebur128` (true peak at or below -1 dBTP; the venue master is chosen at the Mon 22 Feb run; W-19). Run gate G4 (pacing, variety, continuity, captions). Its checks are split (W-42): gated are MO8a/MO8b, captions inside the 90% box, -1 dBTP, colour-patch, L-label present, the AI-tells sweep including the Hindu tells, one still-still, the strangers' first question, and a `card_text` scan for the eight darshan names (PR16, W-62); reported, not gated until G-DRAFT (Sun 31 Jan) are shot-length CoV of 0.4 or more, move-family mix, holds of 35% or more, ΔE00 thresholds, half and double speed, sound-off and picture-off. Its class-A rule: no more than three class-A shots in a row without a held still, a class B, C or D shot, or a voice-only beat; at least one shot per chapter where the photo does not move at all. Gate G5 is dignity: Fable flags in writing, the maker decides under one-key-three-guards, and an agent never passes it (D-04). Run gate G6 (technical QC, including a TV test at about 3 metres). For the phone files PH1 and PH2 (W-63) it adds: ffprobe fields and frame count equal to the timeline; `moov` before `mdat`; twelve colour patches within ±3/255 on the PC build; true peak after the AAC encode; baked-grain survival of 70% or more on PH1; type floors and strokes; the safe-area overlay on three frames; no metadata; a neutral code as the file name before the reveal. PH1 is encoded from the ProRes master (never the venue file) at CRF 20 with an 8 Mb/s cap, and PH2 is 1280x720 at CRF 22 with a 3.5 Mb/s cap (W-63; the phone caption is at most 32 characters a line, W-18), both SDR and not HEVC. The register wins over every package file: `pipeline/phone/profile.json` and `PHONE-ROW.md` must carry those figures (`tests/integration` `parity.phone_profile_vs_register` checks it). If they differ, regenerate the profile or have the orchestrator amend the register, and never paste `PHONE-ROW.md` over the settings card while they disagree. Make them with `bash pipeline/phone/encode_phone.sh MASTER.mov CAPTIONS.srt OUT.mp4` (captions burned in through the `ass` filter with relative file names), check them with `python3 -I pipeline/validators/spec_check.py OUT.mp4 --profiles-file pipeline/phone/profile.json --profile phone_1080` and `python3 -I pipeline/phone/phone_check.py OUT.mp4` (five views: upright, sideways, small phone, after sharing, filled to a 20:9 screen), and read the caption bottom edge in `--scenario fill_20x9`: it must sit at or above 88% of the picture height, because a phone set to fill crops about 10% off the top and the bottom. The venue caption (`FontSize=23`, `MarginV` 20) ends at 92.6% and fails that check by design; the phone file carries its own caption setting. The phone numbers are proposals (J) until the maker has looked at a real phone (`pipeline/phone/PHONE-TEST.md`).
17. **Commit text only** to the repo, which is public by the maker's choice (D-14, D-26; O-13 is answered): code, briefs, manifests (person IDs, never names, addresses or the name of any specific haveli or temple she attends), reviews, licensed fonts and textures, and vendored libraries. Never commit media. Keep the repo plain Git, with each file under 50 MiB and the repo target under about 200 MB (J). Commit messages and file names must look harmless.
18. **Close the session.** Update manifests and the ledger. Prune drafts: keep the approved render, the last two drafts per shot, and any draft a review cites. Delete the other drafts after 14 days. Keep every `renders.jsonl` line.

## Checklists

**Before rendering:** settings match step 2; brief approved at G0; every input's `cloud` level is set by the maker and allows this use; seed is set; no `Math.random`, `Date.now` or real-time timers in the scene; fonts and textures have licence files; spike (SP1 to SP5) results for this route are recorded.

**Validators (before any hand-off):**
- Video (all of the rows below in one command: `python3 -I pipeline/validators/spec_check.py FILE --profile NAME`, profiles in `pipeline/settings.json`, `--list-profiles` lists them): `ffprobe -v error -show_entries stream=codec_name,profile,pix_fmt,width,height,r_frame_rate,avg_frame_rate,nb_frames,color_space,color_range,color_primaries,color_transfer -of json <file>`. The frame rate must equal the project rate. H.264 must show yuv420p, BT.709 and `tv` range. The frame count must equal duration times frame rate. `ffmpeg -v error -i <file> -f null -` must print nothing, and the `moov` atom must be present.
- Alpha: the pixel format includes `a` for ProRes 4444 (`yuva444p12le` was reported in testing, M12).
- Colour matrix (pixels, not tags): encode a 3-patch frame (pure red 255,0,0; 50% grey 128,128,128; skin tone 224,172,140) through the shot's exact command line, decode it to `rgb24` with `scale=in_color_matrix=bt709:in_range=tv:out_range=pc,format=rgb24`, and require every channel within ±3/255. Run it once per command-line template, and again whenever the ffmpeg version differs (the PC build is ffmpeg 9.0.2 from winget, not the container's 6.1.1). Tags alone are not evidence (research note, Q-F1): `python3 -I pipeline/validators/colour_patch.py --chain-file pipeline/lib/chains.json --all` runs the test for every chain and `spec_check.py` does not replace it (a BT.601 encode under BT.709 tags passes every tag check).
- Motion: `python3 -I pipeline/validators/mo8_gate.py FRAMES_OR_CLIP` gates the push at 0.05 px (MO8a) and 0.04 px (MO8b) on the master before the delivery encode, at most 96 frames; a sub-pixel (transform) push passes, a `zoompan` or layout-animated push fails (proved in `tests/integration`).
- SVG: renders non-blank; every ID is present and matches the pattern; `check_ids.py` exits 0 on original and touched files.
- ORA: zip opens; `stack.xml` parses; every `src` exists; layers sit inside the canvas; names are unique and match the ID pattern.
- OTIO: parses with `opentimelineio` (M16); every media target exists; clips sit inside their available range; the rate matches the project.
- Audio: 48000 Hz, 24-bit; record loudness and true peak with `ffmpeg -i <file> -af ebur128=peak=true -f null -`; true peak at or below -1 dBTP (W-19); the phone files at -16 LUFS integrated and -1 dBTP measured on the final MP4 after the AAC encode.
- Phone floor: every meaning line 4 px or more at 1080 (decoration 3, dots and repeat units 6, holes 4), no hairlines; must-read type x-height 3.2% of picture height as the hard floor, 3.8% as the target; hands and the object inside the central 80% of height; text box 5 to 95% across and 12 to 88% down (W-38 decision 3, W-63).
- Deity and darshan: `deity_form` and `deity_source` filled on any shot that carries a deity symbol or a seva object, no generative route touched a deity, and a `card_text` scan finds none of the eight darshan names on any card (PR16, W-62).
- Fonts: OFL only; each family has a T8 `font` row with its `OFL.txt` hash and the libass `fontselect:` line pasted (W-21).
- Lottie (interchange only): reject `layers: []`, and check opacity on a 0 to 100 scale.
- Repo: `python3 -I scripts/public_lint.py` (is it safe to publish; the diff against HEAD plus untracked files) and `python3 -I scripts/stale_check.py --new-only` (no retired value, such as a 25 fps default, that the baseline does not already know) before any commit.
- Determinism: render two frames again and compare with `compare -metric AE -fuzz 1%`. Exact hashes are expected without SVG filters. With filters, accept up to 100 differing pixels per frame (M10).

**Before anything goes to the cloud:** the maker set the `cloud` level; the account's model-improvement setting is off before the first `preview` (otherwise transcripts are kept 5 years and may be used for training, versus 30 days when it is off) [51]; only non-family images, or proxies (about 768 px) of a set the maker raised to `preview`, are used; no hosted renderer or memorial-video service; API keys are in the environment's network secrets [49].

**Redaction and what may be filed (build plan 4.13, 4.4, 4.16):** what counts as identifying data (an e-mail, an IP or MAC address, a profile path, a token, a serial or long hex run, a social handle, and the user, computer or domain name of the machine) is defined once, in `scripts/redaction_patterns.json`, and every scanner reads that file and none carries a copy of an expression: `scripts/public_lint.py`, `scripts/windows_check.py`, `win/step0_pc_tests.ps1` and `win/kit2/run_checks_pc.ps1`. A scanner that cannot read the file fails closed (exit 2, nothing written). `python3 -I scripts/public_lint.py --self-test --fail-on-skip` must show all four consumers agreeing. A hit is printed as its category, its place and the first two characters only. **Raw versus derived:** a raw `pc_report.json` or raw `colour_patch_pc.json` from the maker's PC is a hardware fingerprint of one machine in a public repository. The maker reads it and pastes it in chat only; it is never saved in the project folder, never committed, never filed under `build/evidence/`, and files with those names are refused by `public_lint.py` outside `tests/win/fixtures/`. The only forms that may be filed are the derived ones, made container-side from the pasted text: `python3 -I scripts/windows_check.py --tier-json-out PATH RAW` (`pc_tier.json`: buckets and verdict words, no model names, no exact sizes) and `python3 -I pipeline/validators/colour_patch.py --derive-from RAW --out DERIVED` (`pc_chains.json`: versions, binary hash, chain results, no configure flags, no paths), both under `build/evidence/pc/`.

**Repo hygiene:** the repo is public (D-26): no names, addresses or the name of any specific haveli or temple she attends, only IDs (`P-001`); the key that maps IDs to names stays offline; no media; innocuous repo and commit names. Places and traditions in general are fine.

## Quality bar

**Good:**
- Each shot names its source, and its beat comes from her life, not from a template.
- Motion uses several families, with varied easing and holds of 0.75 s or more after each payoff. Nothing loops or stretches to fill a slot.
- Two renders match (or fall within the stated tolerance). Faces come from the photo and are never regenerated.
- The palette has six colours or fewer, sampled from her prints. Every font has its licence file.
- Validators are green, with JSON attached, and `render.json` is complete.
- The maker has watched the shot with sound on.

**Mediocre (fix before review):**
- Default easing, an evenly spaced stagger, one stock transition, centred typewriter titles, the same zoom on every photo, or neon-on-dark gradients.
- Validators pass but nobody has listened.
- Full-frame live SVG filters, which are slow and not exactly repeatable (1.2 fps and 5 of 36 frames differing in M10).
- The critic approves a shot that the maker has not watched.

## Ethics and privacy guardrails

- **Real memories stay real.** Do not invent events, people, faces or words. Reconstructions are visibly stylised and labelled with their level on the ladder in `research/ai-imagery.md` [2]. The label appears in the manifest, the file name and the credits.
- **No photoreal generation of people, no voice cloning, no AI-sung songs, no AI voice of any devotional text** (W-79). Use her handwriting as a font only for words she actually wrote [1]. No synthetic or cloned voice of any real person, even with consent (D-16), and no AI animation of a real face.
- **Consent level is the maker's decision.** The default is `none`. An agent may not raise a `cloud` level, and may not treat "pending" consent as cleared. Rights tiers follow W-46: nobody is asked before the premiere, so Tier C stays out and Tier B is documented risk ratified after (W-46, W-48).
- **No deity from any generative route** (W-61). A deity is not drawn, generated or photographed in the premiere; only symbols and seva objects that are not a svarupa appear, and AI never touches a deity in any class. Nothing in her seva space is photographed or touched to get a shot (W-70, PR4).
- **Surprise.** Keep media and names out of the repo. Keep repo names, commit messages and folder names harmless. Assume phone notifications can show on shared screens. Keep the project in its own browser profile.
- **Dignity.** Loss, illness, separation, money and conflict go through one-key-three-guards at G5: a decision sheet per item, Fable's written flag, the maker's two decisions 72 hours apart, default OUT of the public cut on any doubt. Anything HOLD or PRIVATE, and anything she might not want seen in front of a public room, stays out of the public cut (charter principle 5; D-04) [1]. The maker is the proxy; nobody else reviews (D-03).
- **Permissions and money.** No unattended multi-agent runs and no bypass-permission studios. One agent, one shot, at most three in parallel, two revision rounds. One unattended run cost $207.58 for 90 seconds [38].
- **Control ports.** Start a Craft app's control port only for the session that needs it, then close it. PhotoCraft requires a token. VectorCraft and LightCraft do not authenticate [4]. A cloud session cannot reach the maker's PC loopback ports [47][50].
- **Computer use.** The CLI cannot click apps on Windows; only the Desktop app can [48]. Use the Craft apps' own channels, files, or the maker's hands.
