# Life Film Story Structure

## Purpose

Keep the Life Journey film structurally honest, followable by strangers, and within the material that really exists. This skill turns `research/film-story.md` into a procedure for agents. The human version is `playbooks/film-story.md`.

The maker is the only person on the project and the only keeper of the secret (answer given 2026-10-18). Do not assume a second keeper, a co-reviewer or an insider (D-03; the one re-open point is TD-L2c, Sun 3 Jan 2027). `DECISIONS.md` is the source of truth; this file was reconciled with it on 2026-10-19, after the her-world merge. Her world shapes the grammar (D-28, D-31): cards in Gujarati with captions in the room's language (W-35, W-75); a cold open on the first act of her morning, a sound above about 400 Hz with a first light within about 4 s (W-36, W-76); an ending in the order of W-69; the hum in two takes (W-65); the darshan hour as a kitchen light and never a life clock (W-62); festivals that open or frame a module and never structure the film (W-77); no deity drawn, generated or photographed and no seva article handled or moved (W-61, W-70).

## When to use

- A treatment, beat sheet, paper edit, storyboard, narration plan, runtime plan or ending is drafted, reviewed or locked.
- A beat is proposed for S3 (drafted) or S5 (locked).
- A runtime change is proposed (6, 9, 12 or 15 to 18 minutes; 25, 45 and 90 are not production targets).
- Story text, title cards, captions or a contributor credit is requested for any chapter.

## When not to use

- Music, score, sound mix or cue choice in detail (the music-sound track owns these).
- Rendering, animation, photo restoration or app work.
- Choosing, downloading or watching exemplar films. The maker checks legal routes.
- Any request for her words, new scenes of her, or a cloned or re-cut voice. Refuse and explain (see Ethics).

## Procedure

1. Read `CHARTER.md` and the playbook section your task touches. Confirm the sole-keeper status first.
2. Check the runtime state (W-01 to W-05, D-06, D-24). 12 minutes (wrapper plus three 3:00 modules) is the target if the maker's hours H are at least 16 a week; 9 if H is below 14; 14 to 16 plans 12 only if the maker adds hours or buys captions; 15 to 18 is a stretch only at G-SLICE (Sun 20 Dec), with 18 the hard cap; F-0 is 6 minutes ("object and photos", no voice). There is no 25-minute private cut: the April version is the same locked picture with voices. Do not plan 45 or 90 minutes. Until the maker writes H (due Sun 15 Nov, O-19), plan 9 and mark each output "runtime pending H."
3. Accept only IDs as input: a beat row, its evidence IDs, the quote file and the style bible. Refuse prose requests such as "write a scene about her wedding," and ask for the evidence IDs.
4. Build structure from functions, not events: COLD_OPEN, PLACE, OBJECT, TUESDAY, TURN, TEXTURE, RELIEF, HARD_GLIMPSE, TIME_PASS, CHAPTER_CARD, EMPTY_CHAPTER, ENDING. Keep every slot at S0 (empty) or S1 (evidence promised) until evidence exists. Festivals and samskara markers are overlays keyed to `kept_by_her` and to evidence (W-77): a festival may open or frame a module and never structures the film (W-39); the eight darshans set a kitchen's light and a cue's hour and never a life stage, an era, a chapter or a card (W-62).
5. Write each slot as an evidence request with a fallback card, for example "1 image of first home + 1 sense detail + 1 witness line; fallback: 'no photograph survives'." Never write a fact before a source gives it.
6. Pick the narration source by the ladder: HER-PUBLIC (only if it exists and is decided under the 72-hour double decision and the written "would she play this to strangers herself" line), then WIT (4 to 6 named tellers at most, verbatim, as attributed text with no voice in the premiere cut, D-05), then CARD (quoted source), then SND (wordless with score). HER-PRIVATE (voicemail, voice note, answering machine) never enters the public cut and is post-reveal only (W-45, surprise-ops Decision 18). MAK-CARD is allowed for the contract card and for plain statements of what the maker knows or does not know (gap cards); never for her feelings or inner life; capped at about 10 percent of runtime (balance check 4). MAK-VOICE is the hum (two takes: closed-mouth for the hall if the room carries it, open vowel for the phone, W-65) and at most one plain factual fragment tested in the slice and kept at G-SLICE (W-35). Cards are in Gujarati with room-language captions (W-35, W-75). Never use a neutral narrator or "I am her" narration. For any FOUND_VOICE beat (HER-PUBLIC or HER-PRIVATE), four inventory fields are mandatory: addressee, knowing recording, sensitivity (health, money, conflict or a third party), and other voices on the recording; a blank field keeps the beat at S1.
7. Fill the claims table (Checklist B) for every card, caption and board frame. A claim without an ID is UNSUPPORTED, and its frame stays a grey SLOT. No beat reaches S3 with an UNSUPPORTED claim. Show a CONTRADICTED claim as "X remembers," or cut it.
8. Draft the paper edit in columns: asset ID; quote or card text with its source; evidence ID; consent status. Run the radio test: does the chapter work with the eyes closed?
9. Storyboard only from IDs. Each frame cites an ID or is SLOT. Each L5 (reconstruction) frame cites a testimony quote ID and carries the caption "Reconstructed from [name]'s memory, 2026."
10. Apply the inversion rule to uneven material. Give scarce early images long holds, one per chapter at most. For the recent decade, use an 8 to 10 second burst or one ordinary day. Allocate time by chapter, with no era above about 25 percent of runtime. Say the gap once, as a true card. Caption any context image that is not hers.
11. Handle hard chapters by trait, not incident, under one-key-three-guards (D-04). Default to OUT for the public cut on any doubt. Fable writes a flag first (Fable flags and never passes G5). Record two decisions at least 72 hours apart as maker-proxy-72h-1 and maker-proxy-72h-2. Both must say yes. The public cut holds no HOLD or PRIVATE item.
12. Lock a chapter (S5) only when all six rules in Checklist C pass. Lock in order of evidence richness, not chronology. Design each transition for both neighbours at lock time.
13. Set the cold open by W-36 and W-76: black, then the first act of her morning as a sound above about 400 Hz chosen from the measured ladder on the project Android (water poured into brass if the home keeps Yamunajal in a jhari, a stand-in vessel and never her jhari; the household bell if the morning bell is hers; else a spoon on steel or the cooker), a visible first light within about 4 s at the 05:30 hour, no rhythm instrument, no card, no name; room tone alone is never a first sound; then hands and the object in use, the hum under, the laugh, the contract card in the maker's hand ("[Name]. What is drawn is marked."), the title last. Run the stranger test on the treatment's first two minutes, and again on the animatic's first two minutes. Readers must never have met her, and must answer exit questions 1 to 4 of the who-is-she brief. If they cannot, rewrite. Stranger-read copies substitute a code name for her and `[relation]` tokens for every named teller; the exit test asks whether a name was said by someone who loves her and who in relation is telling it, not which name; real names live only in the private repo.
14. Set the ending from the card: 9 and 12 minutes "the trace and the open chapter", in the order of W-69 (a held EMPTY_CHAPTER card of 6 to 10 s in her name in native Gujarati; the object set down by the maker's hands; the hum returning; the hichko seat, keyed `evening`, only if she has one and sits in it; the last card "જય શ્રી કૃષ્ણ" alone, only if her family says it; the room's own sound; hard cut to black; credits with the cue sheet; the 1:45 ending is itemised in the playbook, Template 8; W-36, W-37, W-67), and the same ending for a 15 to 18 minute stretch. 25 minutes is retired (D-06) and 45 minutes "the return" is not planned (D-24). The open-chapter card goes in at any runtime unless the maker, as proxy, decides it does not suit her. End on a held card and house lights. Never end on a shot that frames the room, a second-person address to her, or a request that she react.
15. Log every generative element, music cue and archival source to the cue sheet and credits log as it enters the film.
16. Report: slot states, balance-check results, counts of UNSUPPORTED and CONTRADICTED claims, every TODO(research) item the output touches, and any decision the maker must make.

## Checklists

**A. Before a beat moves from S2 to S3**
- [ ] evidence_ids filled (empty only at S0 or S1)
- [ ] no identifiable third party who is heard still reads "not-asked"
- [ ] narration_src assigned and traceable
- [ ] borrowed_licence = NONE
- [ ] no UNSUPPORTED rows in the claims table
- [ ] humour comes from a found or witness line and sits beside a loss, not on it
- [ ] every L5 frame cites a testimony quote ID and carries the caption
- [ ] `motif_check` filled and not `excluded`; a `flagged` beat has gone to Fable; `political_room` set on any beat for Somnath, Dwarka or Ayodhya (W-66, W-71)

**B. Balance checks (on every save)**
1. Seconds per era, and each era's share against the 25 percent starting cap.
2. Density: seconds per year of her life, per chapter.
3. Hard beats: count them; the first comes no earlier than 25 to 33 percent of runtime.
4. Narration mix: MAK-VOICE only the hum and at most one factual fragment passed at G-SLICE (W-35); MAK-CARD at about 10 percent of runtime or less.
5. Label mix: L5 seconds per chapter, each with a testimony ID.
6. Unlocked consent: any not-asked beat with an identifiable third party.
7. Claims: any unsupported claim.
8. Hindu-world flags: any beat whose `motif_check` is `flagged`, `excluded`, `jain_flag`, `adivasi_flag` or `political-room`; any card that names a darshan (PR16); any festival used to structure rather than open or frame a module (W-77).

Claims table header: `claim_id,beat_id,claim_text,type(name|date|place|number|colour|relationship|quote),evidence_id,status(SUPPORTED|UNSUPPORTED|CONTRADICTED),notes`

**C. Lock a chapter (S5): all six must pass**
- [ ] (a) every beat has evidence IDs or an L5 testimony ID
- [ ] (b) no identifiable third party who is heard reads "not-asked"
- [ ] (c) the hard-chapter flag was decided twice, 72 hours apart, both yes
- [ ] (d) narration source assigned and traceable
- [ ] (e) stranger-read passed for the chapter
- [ ] (f) runtime inside budget

**D. Public-cut content check**
- [ ] no beat reveals a secret, a hidden relative or an estrangement to the room
- [ ] no beat makes the room look at her; no cue to applaud
- [ ] the ending needs nothing from her; no second-person address
- [ ] every chapter is a module with a visible card before the next
- [ ] the run sheet plans for refusal as a normal outcome, not an edge case (surprise-ops owns the lines)

**E. Runtime budgets (including end credits; W-04)**
- 12 min: wrapper plus three 3:00 modules: cold open 1:15; three modules of 3:00; ending 1:45 (open chapter card 0:08, object and hum 0:30, hichko seat 0:12, last card 0:05, room sound 0:08, black 0:05, credits 0:37; without the hichko seat or the last card their seconds go to the object and the hum; W-69).
- 9 min: wrapper plus two 3:00 modules: cold open 1:15; two modules of 3:00; ending 1:45.
- 6 min (F-0, no voice): cold open 1:15; one module of 3:00; ending 1:45.
- 15 to 18 min (stretch only at G-SLICE): wrapper 3:00 (cold open 1:15 plus ending 1:45); four or five modules of 3:00.
- 25 min (retired, D-06), 45 min and 90 min: not planned (D-24).

**F. Beat row header (use exactly)**

`beat_id,time_start_s,dur_target_s,dur_actual_s,chapter,device,evidence_ids,label_L,consent_status,hard_flag,narration_src,music_cue,slot_state,function,evidence_need,sound_state,humour,stranger_test,borrowed_structure,borrowed_licence,unknowns,place_id,political_room,motif_check,samskara_or_festival,rasa`

Allowed values: label_L is L0 to L5 (L4a/b/c for animated); hard_flag is NONE, SHARED-LIGHT, PRIVATE, HOLD or OUT; narration_src is HER-PUBLIC, HER-PRIVATE, WIT:<name>, CARD:<quote id>, MAK-CARD, MAK-VOICE or SND; slot_state is S0 to S5; device is OBJECT_CLOCK, PORTRAIT_LADDER, LETTER, SCALE_ZOOM, LONG_HOLD, BLACK_FRAME, TUESDAY, WITNESS_LINE, FOUND_VOICE (the HER split applies), TEXT_CARD, STAND_IN_L5, ARCHIVE_REAL or GAME_GRAMMAR. GAME_GRAMMAR (an object-ledger reveal, a map that fills in, a trophies-aside reveal, a pause chime) is allowed only when it passes the swap-the-subject test and is defined in playbooks/games.md; no game's footage, look or assets in the public cut (D-29, review 02, 0.6). `place_id` is P01 to P10 or F1 to F3 from `playbooks/saurashtra-world.md`, or blank; `political_room` is blank or the flag that playbook sets for Somnath, Dwarka and Ayodhya (W-71); `motif_check` is `hindu`, `Hindu-Gujarati`, `flagged`, `excluded`, `jain_flag`, `adivasi_flag` or `political-room` (W-66; `excluded` blocks S3, `flagged` goes to Fable); `samskara_or_festival` is a `marker_id` from `playbooks/hindu-frameworks.md` Template A, filled only with evidence and, for a festival, `kept_by_her` yes (W-77); `rasa` is the module's dominant rasa from Template B, never a bhava assigned to her.

## Quality bar: theatrical or mediocre

Theatrical:
- The first two minutes pass the exit test using a first sound above about 400 Hz and a first light, an object and a laugh, with a name written or said by someone who loves her arriving with the contract card (W-36, W-76), not a CV.
- Every word and frame traces to an ID, and the film says what it does not know.
- The sincere version of a moment is shorter, quieter and later than the showy one.
- The hard life lives in an object or a withheld image, not in a shown incident.
- The ending needs nothing from her, and the house lights come up.
- The cut is shorter than the material could support, and each scene earns its place.

Mediocre:
- A reel or slideshow: photos on a timer with captions and no sound design.
- Narrator irony, or a maker monologue doing the feeling for her.
- Invented scenes, composite people or words in her mouth, however moving.
- Decade clichés (Cooper Black, avocado appliances, heavy grain) in place of her own scans.
- An in-joke only the family gets, or laughter at her.
- Padding with generic material to reach a length.

## Ethics, rights and privacy guardrails

- Real memories stay real. AI may restore, restyle, animate and connect real photos and stories. It must not invent events or people, put words in anyone's mouth, or clone a voice (charter, principle 4).
- Never: ask her for "a few words" under a pretext; clone or synthesise her voice; re-cut her found speech into new sentences; record her without consent; have a relative read as her; have a narrator say what she felt; animate her face to speak words she did not say; generate new scenes of her; use a live-action shot of her taken without her knowledge.
- Pretexts must be truthful. Under only-me no relative is told that a film is being made (D-03), and nobody is read the consent card (card 4a) before the premiere; it is used for release 4d after the premiere (W-48). Use the truthful pretext cards in surprise-ops [P3].
- Consent values: asked-ok, asked-ok-with-limits, not-asked (surprise hold), declined, third-party-needs-check, bottom-drawer (do not use).
- Keep all private material in the private repository. Do not upload anything to a third-party service, including vendor paper-edit tools, without the maker's explicit say (charter).
- Music, photos taken by others, archival footage, fonts and the voices of people who appear must be cleared for public screening (charter, principle 7).
- Do not print a quotation until checked against the original; the research note marks unopened sources "snippet only".
- Generative elements go on the cue sheet and in the credits [61].
- No deity is drawn, generated or photographed, and no seva article is handled, moved, dissolved or used as a transition (W-61, W-70, PR10); the home mandir's doors appear once, closed, in the shrine moment. Mahaprabhuji is a symbol, static, framed high, never captioned casually (PR19). No card names a darshan (PR16), assigns a bhava to her or uses "Mataji" for her; a garlanded photograph is never of her (W-78).
- Hindu-only: every motif, text, sound, instrument, craft, place and name carries a `motif_check`; shared or ambiguous origin is out by default and the maker overrules one line at a time in a log (W-66).
