# GATHER: what to collect, and when

*Orchestrator's checklist for the maker. IDs only: this repository is public (D-26), so nothing here names a person, a place or an address. Write answers in your own private notes (your phone, never synced) and put only the ID and a done-date in the blanks below. The ID key stays offline with you.*

**ID shapes used on this page.** `ERA-1` to `ERA-5` (the five placeholder eras of W-39) · `HUM-01` (your hum) · `OBJ-01` to `OBJ-05` (object-clock candidates) · `REC-01`, `REC-02`, ... (existing voice recordings) · `FP-01` to `FP-10` (family-profile lines) · `DEV-01`, `DEV-02`, ... (shared devices and accounts) · `LJ-<box>-<nnnn>-F` / `-B` (a scan, front / back; box code `ST` is reserved for stand-ins so a stand-in can never be mistaken for a real item). People are `[relation]` tokens or `P-001`-style IDs, never names.

## Part B: From Sun 15 Nov, only after P-LOCK passes

**Gate: nothing in Part B starts until P-LOCK (Sat 14 Nov) has passed and the date is Sun 15 Nov or later (D-13, W-06).** The gathering window runs Sun 15 Nov to Sat 19 Dec for outgoing asks; all media is back by Sun 24 Jan and gathering stops on Sat 30 Jan (W-10).

### B1. Item classes

Each item you gather becomes one row in the inventory sheet (not on this page) with these fields: `id`, `class`, `holder` (a relation token only), `cloud` (default `none`; only you may raise it), `consent_state`, `allowed_use`, `as_found_photographed` (y/n), `scanned` (y/n), `returned` (y/n).

Allowed values: `consent_state` is `not-asked` (nobody is asked before the premiere, D-03) or `release-pending` (releases go out after it, W-48); `allowed_use` is `hall`, `private-link`, `family-only` or `none` (W-31).

### B2. The rules, one line each

- **Covert (D-02):** nothing may tip her off; a shared device, shared library or group chat is a leak.
- **Gate (D-13):** no real material is touched before the Part B gate above.
- **Privacy (D-15):** personal media stays in `media/` and on the encrypted drive; `cloud: none`; nothing goes to a third-party service without your explicit OK.
- **Never record her (D-17):** no recording of her without her knowledge and a spoken yes; the announced evening with a visible recorder and the wide shot at the reveal are the only planned cases.
- **Album-and-hands (W-49):** a phone visibly filming only the hands and the pages, under one true sentence, with a spoken "fine" on the file; the face is never in frame.
- **Hard items (D-04):** anything that touches loss, illness, separation, money or conflict gets a decision sheet, a written Fable flag and your two decisions 72 hours apart; out of the public cut on any doubt.
- **Borrow log:** record every item taken, by ID and holder token, before it leaves its place.
- **Return the same day:** the item goes back before she or its holder would miss it, and `returned` is set to y.
