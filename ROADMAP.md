## 1. North star and the two films

We are making a short animated documentary of one woman's life, built to a cinema standard, for a room that mostly has never met her, with her sitting in it and not knowing it was coming. It is a film first: 1920x1080 at 24 fps, a real sound mix, a proper master, projected on Saturday 27 February 2027 and kept for twenty years on a drive she can open. Every device on screen is drawn from her own archive and habits: the as-found prints with the writing on their backs, the one object that followed her through every home, hands holding each thing a chapter is told through, the material her own hands made standing in for everything never photographed, the chapters only she can tell left visibly open. Nothing is invented: AI restores, restyles, animates and connects her real photographs; it invents no events, no faces, no voices. Game-world language appears only where a mechanic says something true (D-23).

**The maker is the only person on the project** (D-03). That one fact designs two films:

| Film | Date | What it is | Who speaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The premiere cut** | Sat 27 Feb 2027 | 9 or 12 minutes (Section 3), the film as strangers meet her: what she kept, whose hands hold it, her own found voice where one recording passes, cards in the maker's hand, sound from her world. Ethically clean by construction: nothing in it was gathered under a pretext from someone who did not know. | No recorded voice of any living person except her own HER-PUBLIC clip if one passes one-key-three-guards (D-05). |
| **The April version** | Sat 24 Apr 2027 | The same locked picture with the voices turned on: each relative who has seen the film and signed release 4d records one memory in response to it; her own coda, recorded only with her spoken yes after the her-turn conversation of Sat 6 Mar, closes it. Only the dialogue stem and the mix change (D-06). | The people who were there, and last of all her. |

Success test (review 00, kept): she recognises herself in details nobody else would have thought to include; a mixed-age room follows it with no instructions; strangers leave with specific questions for her rather than compliments for the maker; it still plays in 2047.

## 2. What we learned: ten lessons that change how we build

1. **"Pipeline locked" is a process lock in week 4, not the week-9 slice.** P-LOCK on Sat 14 Nov, proven on synthetic material; covert gathering starts Sun 15 Nov; everything that needs no gate (secrecy set-up, lab emails, the maker's own practice, casting the object) starts now. — `playbooks/surprise-ops.md` Decision 0; `playbooks/production-scale.md` schedule model.
2. **Only-me designs two films, and the hole in the surprise is the structure.** The voice the film most needs cannot be recorded before the premiere, so the public cut is a set of kept-by rooms, an open chapter in her name, three locked rooms, and a reserved coda slot in `timeline.json`; the April version is where the voices live. — `playbooks/film-story.md` Template 8, Decision 7; `playbooks/theatrical-delivery.md` Step 11.
3. **The look comes from her house, not a style menu.** Every print enters as found (`L00_asfound`) and is restored in front of the audience; the measured border cast of each batch (`border_rgb`) is the palette; the hand-made material for the unphotographed is whatever her hands actually made; the look changes where the photographer changes. — `playbooks/ai-imagery.md` Step 10; `playbooks/craft-visual.md` Template C; `playbooks/cinematic-quality.md` T2.
4. **Hands are the grammar; the object is the clock.** Every chapter opens on hands holding the thing it is told through; whoever's hands hold the picture is telling; the one surviving object is the only transition device, seen first and last, and stands under the screen on the night. The camera never searches for a face. — `playbooks/film-story.md` principle 5, Template 5.
5. **Teach the grammar once, break each rule exactly once.** Three honest textures keyed in the first minute; hold length tracks the gap since the previous photograph; cutting rate tracks how settled her life was; one "still still" per chapter is a photograph she took; the single bend is the turning point of her life. — `playbooks/creator-threads.md` Part D; `playbooks/cinematic-quality.md` step 14; `playbooks/games.md` principle 5.
6. **Agents generate, apps refine; scripts travel, photos stay home.** Route A drawn scenes render in the container, every full-size photo renders on the PC from the same script; each photo set carries `cloud: none` unless the maker raises it; the Craft apps touch copies in `work\` folders under pinned portable zips and nothing critical depends on one. — `playbooks/agent-pipeline.md` Steps 1–14; `playbooks/toolchain.md` Decision 5.
7. **Runtime follows hours and the material, with a trigger date.** 12 minutes needs about 16 hours a week under only-me (lever 3 off); if H is written under 14 by Sun 15 Nov the plan is 9 from that day, not from 20 Dec; the slice's measured `h_slice` confirms on Sun 20 Dec. — `playbooks/production-scale.md` T1, T2, Kill-or-cut.
8. **"Theatrical" is quality per minute, and the stranger in row seven is the first reader.** Ten style-bible decisions before the slice; a pass/fail test on a projected frame for every per-shot claim; two screenings with strangers-to-her whose first question is "if you met her afterwards, what would you ask her?"; the 48-hour review and blind A/B because there is no editor. — `playbooks/cinematic-quality.md` Steps 2, 15–16, T7.
9. **A public room means cleared on every copy, and the venue decides the master.** Sync, master and public-performance rights for every copy; OFL fonts only; the venue asked in writing by Fri 6 Nov and its answer, due Sun 15 Nov, decides file or DCP; every Tier B or C appearance has an understudy before lock so a withdrawal is a swap, never a re-edit. — `playbooks/theatrical-delivery.md` T1, T8, TD-1–TD-8; `playbooks/music-sound.md` Steps 6, 10–11.
10. **Honesty labelling is intrinsic.** Six levels L0–L5 travel in the file name, `steps.csv`, XMP and the cue sheet; if it looks like a photograph it is one; anything invented is drawn in her material; no synthetic voice of anyone, no AI animation of a real face, no model trained on her face. — `playbooks/ai-imagery.md` principles 1, 6–7; `playbooks/animated.md` Pitfalls.

## 3. The film

### 3.1 Format

**Option A** (W-30): a linear, captioned, photo-forward film in chapters projected to the room, plus the **offline scrubber page** emitted from the same `timeline.json` on the USB she takes home. Live interludes only if the maker says playable is a must *and* G-SLICE passes with hours to spare *and* ten clean dry runs by Sun 17 Jan. A scroll story (option D) only as a private-link companion after the premiere. Full matrix: `playbooks/format.md` Decision 1.

### 3.3 Structure and narration

- **Wrapper plus kept-by modules** (W-04): each module is one place, one object, one device, one turn, opened on the as-found frame ("this is where she keeps 1988") and on hands (`hands_owner`, relation only). The `teller` field becomes `teller (April version)` and `material (premiere)`.
- **Eras as biomes** (W-39): 0–12, 13–22, 23–35, 36–49, 50+, each with its own colour strip and "who held the camera"; no era above about 25% of runtime. Density is a portrait: dense decades cut fast (about 12 shots a minute), sparse decades hold on one visibly reconstructed L5 image and say the gap out loud once (`shots_per_min` derived from `material_in_hand_pct`).
- **The object clock** (W-32): cast from her present in week 1 with no relative asked, traced backwards through the albums, seen in the first and last thirty seconds.
- **Five hero moments in one room** (W-34): the only class C shots; chosen with the stranger's question first; **the kitchen is the only built set**, one Blender set rendered at a different hour and decade each time, never explained. Four at 9 minutes.
- **Locked rooms and the open chapter** (W-37): three visibly unfinished places from the coverage strip's dark stretches; a held card, "Chapter [N]: [Her name]. To be told by her."
- **Two doors** (W-31): every shot carries `allowed_use` (`hall`, `private-link`, `family-only`, `none`); the hall cut, the family-key cut and her cut are emitted from one timeline with no re-edit.
- **Narration stack for the premiere cut** (W-35): 1. HER-PUBLIC, if one clip passes one-key-three-guards. 2. Cards, few and large, in the maker's hand, under 10% of runtime; her handwriting only for words she wrote (the Card A slips). 3. Attributed text with no voice ("[relation] remembers…"), sparingly. 4. Wordless, with sound from her world. The maker's own voice: cards plus the hum by default; one factual "I remember" fragment (5–10 s, about the object) tested in the slice and kept only if the S1 strangers' questions are about her. Never a hired or neutral narrator.
- **Cold open and ending** (W-36): black and one true sound from her kitchen; hands and the object in use; the hummed tune under; her laugh if HER-PUBLIC passed, else a found photograph of her laughing, held; the contract card ("[Name]. What is drawn is marked."); the title last. Ending: the open chapter card, the object where it sits today with the maker's hands setting it down, the hum returning in the maker's plain voice, then the sound of the room the audience sits in, cut to black, credits. "No speech. There's food."
- **Chapter template**: `reviews/archive/ROADMAP.md` §4 (code names only; copy into `story/chapters.md`), with the new fields `hands_owner`, `hero_moment`, `shots_per_min`, `material (premiere)`, `teller (April version)`.

### 3.4 Look and the reconstruction marker

The style bible's ten decisions lock at G1 (Sun 15 Nov) on the maker's own material (W-38): the hand-made material is **whatever her hands actually made** (answered by the maker in week 1, O-06; one swap allowed on Sun 13 Dec, budget 6 hours); the reconstruction marker follows ("the parts nobody photographed are shown in the stuff she made", labelled L5 with the caption "Reconstructed from [relation]'s memory, [date]"); OFL fonts only; camera grammar adds the hands rule and "one room only"; grain and paper from the backs of her prints; titles from the Card A slips traced in VectorCraft (Inkscape fallback). ## 4. Operating model under only-me

### 4.1 The hard-chapter process (D-04)
One-key-three-guards: a decision sheet per candidate item (`surprise-ops` template, single key); Fable's written flag in `rights/flags/<asset_id>.md`; the maker's two decisions 72 hours apart (Fri 5 Feb is the last second decision); default OUT of the public cut on any doubt. Anything HOLD or PRIVATE is out of the public cut, full stop. The her-turn conversation (Sat 6 Mar) is the only way back in. Fable flags and never passes G5. The maker's "cold reads" replace the old proxy viewings: the rough public cut Sun 31 Jan, the locked cut Sun 7 Feb, the private cut (the family-key cut emitted from `timeline.json`; `DECISIONS.md` Codes) Sun 14 Feb, each after 48 hours unopened with Fable's flags read first.

### 4.2 Secrecy rules (W-26, D-17, D-18, D-19)
Never record her without her knowledge and a spoken yes (the announced Card E evening and the wide shot at the reveal are the only planned cases). Omit, never lie. Nobody is read the consent card before the premiere; relatives are sources through low-key asks and **album-and-hands sessions** (W-49: phone visible, hands and pages only, a spoken "fine" on the file, the voice held as Tier C for release 4d). A separate Windows account; OneDrive off for Desktop, Documents and Pictures; portable Craft zips with data folders beside the program; Recovery folders, recent-file lists, LightCraft caches and XMP sidecars cleared after every session by `lj.ps1 close`, the single session-close command (W-26; Section 5.1); Do Not Disturb during renders; the Mum-test on every shared device now and monthly; the shared-library check before any scan, render or upload ("unknown counts as yes"); working copies named by shot id; test cards only in any bug report (the Craft ticket bot relays to public GitHub); repo, commits, folders and notifications look harmless, person IDs only, the key offline. Slip protocol: stop, assess in ten minutes, write it down within the hour, tell the advisor at the next session, choose carry on / partial reveal / convert / stop, fix the cause, log it. Guests are invited to a gathering, not to a film (Section 11). The only-me question is re-opened once, at TD-L2c on Sun 3 Jan, never silently.

### 4.3 Privacy and cloud policy (D-15, W-24)
`cloud: none` on every photo set by default; only the maker raises it. The account's model-improvement setting is off before the first `preview` proxy (about 768 px). No full-size family photo, interview audio or master in a cloud session, a hosted renderer, a "memorial video" service or any Community Cloud GPU, ever; a rented GPU only for non-family imagery on Secure Cloud with an encrypted volume and the maker's recorded OK. Transcription runs locally. Strangers at S1 and S2 see a redacted copy (code name, `[relation]` tokens).

### 4.4 Storage and backups (D-14, W-25)
Text in git, media on the drive: text, scripts, manifests with IDs, playbooks, skills, `timeline.json`, OTIO, markers and logs commit at the end of every session to the repo (public, so IDs only, never names); scans, audio, video and renders never enter git (no LFS). Media live in a VeraCrypt container on the external drive (mounted only while working, dismounted before leaving the desk) with a second encrypted SSD kept elsewhere; 3-2-1 with yearly hash checks; checksums in the repo; two verified archive copies plus a BD-R read back by Sat 20 Feb; copies refreshed by Feb 2032. A sealed envelope with one trusted person (where the drive and the ID key are, who may be told) opened only if the maker is incapacitated; it brings nobody in (W-13).

## 6. Quality system and every review gate

**Per shot** (`playbooks/cinematic-quality.md` T1; `skills/cinematic-quality-bar/SKILL.md`): a label L0–L5, a `because:`, an ease family, `mirrored: false`, lens and sources; MO1 ease checks; the pixel audits (MO8a ≤0.05 px and MO8b ≤0.04 px on the pre-encode master via `mo8_audit.py`, roughness informational; `pan_ease_classifier.py`, `zoom_velocity_classifier.py`); finish of two layers or fewer with baked seeded grain; type at 5% of picture height, captions `FontSize=23` inside the 90% box; the maker's eye checks and the AI-tells sweep. **Per chapter, gated** (a fail stops G4): MO8a/MO8b on the pre-encode master; captions inside the 90% box; -1 dBTP; the colour-patch JSON; an L-label present on every shot; the AI-tells sweep; one still-still she took; the strangers' first question answered about her. **Per chapter, reported but not gated until G-DRAFT** (written in the pack, read at G4, enforced only from Sun 31 Jan): three or more move families in any eight moving shots, none above 40%; holds at least 35% of photo shots; one picture-led silence of 3 s or more; shot-length coefficient of variation ≥0.4; the ΔE00 thresholds; blind A/B, squint, distance, half and double speed, sound-off and picture-off. **Dailies** (W-42): one review pack per shot that moved, scored twice by a fresh-context critic; the maker watches with sound on for 5–10 minutes, drops markers only (red cut, yellow hold, green breath, blue wrong photo), approves nothing until 48 hours have passed; every Sunday a burn-down, `hours.csv`, `status.csv`, a style-drift check against the frozen G1 frames and one decision-log entry per lock. **Likeness**: the maker alone signs, five of five, twice, 48 hours apart; the features that tipped the choice are logged as the film's leitmotif. **Strangers** (W-43): S1 (one or two strangers to her, remote on the redacted proxy if easier, or the cold self-read with Fable's rubric pass) and S2 (three to five) as below; first question "if you met her at the reception afterwards, what would you ask her?", then the two T7 questions "what does it remind you of?" (a named film or template is a cookie-cutter flag) and "name one thing that could only be about her"; question C ("who is she, in three sentences") kept as a deliverable; pass at ≥80% identifying reconstructions and one person across ages, no unread text, ≤2 tell-marks a minute.
