## 1. North star and the two films

We are making a short animated documentary of one woman's life, built to a cinema standard, for a room that mostly has never met her, with her sitting in it and not knowing it was coming. It is a film first: 1920x1080 at 24 fps, a real sound mix, a proper master, projected on Saturday 27 February 2027 and kept for twenty years on a drive she can open. Every device on screen is drawn from her own archive and habits: the as-found prints with the writing on their backs, the one object that followed her through every home, hands holding each thing a chapter is told through, the material her own hands made standing in for everything never photographed, the chapters only she can tell left visibly open. Nothing is invented: AI restores, restyles, animates and connects her real photographs; it invents no events, no faces, no voices. **In her idiom, it is a day in her Gujarati Pushtimarg house that holds her whole life** (D-28, D-31): Yamunaji's water poured into brass before anyone is awake and one flame against blue-black kitchen glass at the Mangla hour, never named; the kitchen at the hours of the eight darshans, never explained; her hands at their makings opening every chapter; the one domestic object that followed her through every home as the only transition; her evening seat on the hichko to close. Its visual and thematic world is Hindu, hers first: her tradition's floor-art line and the pichwai manner as a frame in season, the goddesses in their own register, Shiva as the linga, the sea and the lamp; the deities as symbols and seva objects, **never a drawn, generated or photographed deity** (W-61, W-46, W-72); cards in Gujarati dated the way her house says a date, captions in the room's language (W-68, W-75). Every one of these choices is keyed to a question only the maker can answer at the Sun 25 Oct sitting (`DECISIONS.md` Table 3); if the answer is no, that piece falls away and nothing else changes.

**The maker is the only person on the project** (D-03). That one fact designs two films:

## 2. What we learned: ten lessons that change how we build

1. **"Pipeline locked" is a process lock in week 4, not the week-9 slice.** P-LOCK on Sat 14 Nov, proven on synthetic material; covert gathering starts Sun 15 Nov; everything that needs no gate (secrecy set-up, lab emails, the maker's own practice, casting the object) starts now. — `playbooks/surprise-ops.md` Decision 0; `playbooks/production-scale.md` schedule model.
2. **Only-me designs two films, and the hole in the surprise is the structure.** The voice the film most needs cannot be recorded before the premiere, so the public cut is a set of kept-by rooms, an open chapter in her name, three locked rooms, and a reserved coda slot in `timeline.json`; the April version is where the voices live. — `playbooks/film-story.md` Template 8, Decision 7; `playbooks/theatrical-delivery.md` Step 11.
3. **The look comes from her house, not a style menu.** Every print enters as found (`L00_asfound`) and is restored in front of the audience; the measured border cast of each batch (`border_rgb`) is the palette; the hand-made material for the unphotographed is whatever her hands actually made; the look changes where the photographer changes. — `playbooks/ai-imagery.md` Step 10; `playbooks/craft-visual.md` Template C; `playbooks/cinematic-quality.md` T2.
4. **Hands are the grammar; the object is the clock.** Every chapter opens on hands holding the thing it is told through; whoever's hands hold the picture is telling; the one surviving object is the only transition device, seen first and last, and stands under the screen on the night. The camera never searches for a face. — `playbooks/film-story.md` principle 5, Template 5.
5. **Teach the grammar once, break each rule exactly once.** Three honest textures keyed in the first minute; hold length tracks the gap since the previous photograph; cutting rate tracks how settled her life was; one "still still" per chapter is a photograph she took; the single bend is the turning point of her life. — `playbooks/creator-threads.md` Part D; `playbooks/cinematic-quality.md` step 14; `playbooks/games.md` principle 5.
6. **Agents generate, apps refine; scripts travel, photos stay home.** Route A drawn scenes render in the container, every full-size photo renders on the PC from the same script; each photo set carries `cloud: none` unless the maker raises it; the Craft apps touch copies in `work\` folders under pinned portable zips and nothing critical depends on one. — `playbooks/agent-pipeline.md` Steps 1–14; `playbooks/toolchain.md` Decision 5.
7. **Runtime follows hours and the material, with a trigger date.** 12 minutes needs about 16 hours a week under only-me (lever 3 off); if H is written under 14 by Sun 15 Nov the plan is 9 from that day, not from 20 Dec; the slice's measured `h_slice` confirms on Sun 20 Dec. — `playbooks/production-scale.md` T1, T2, Kill-or-cut.
8. **"Theatrical" is quality per minute, and the stranger in row seven is the first reader.** Ten style-bible decisions before the slice; a pass/fail test on a projected frame for every per-shot claim; two screenings with strangers-to-her whose first question is "if you met her afterwards, what would you ask her?"; the 48-hour review and blind A/B because there is no editor. — `playbooks/cinematic-quality.md` Steps 2, 15–16, T7.
9. **A public room means cleared on every copy, and the venue decides the master.** Sync, master and public-performance rights for every copy; OFL fonts only; the venue asked in writing by Fri 6 Nov and its answer, due Sun 15 Nov, decides file or DCP; every Tier B or C appearance has an understudy before lock so a withdrawal is a swap, never a re-edit. — `playbooks/theatrical-delivery.md` T1, T8, TD-1–TD-8; `playbooks/music-sound.md` Steps 6, 10–11.
10. **Honesty labelling is intrinsic.** Six levels L0–L5 travel in the file name, `steps.csv`, XMP and the cue sheet; if it looks like a photograph it is one; anything invented is drawn in her material; no synthetic voice of anyone, no AI animation of a real face, no model trained on her face. — `playbooks/ai-imagery.md` principles 1, 6–7; `playbooks/animated.md` Pitfalls.

### 4.2 Secrecy rules (W-26, D-17, D-18, D-19)
Never record her without her knowledge and a spoken yes (the announced Card E evening and the wide shot at the reveal are the only planned cases). Omit, never lie. Nobody is read the consent card before the premiere; relatives are sources through low-key asks and **album-and-hands sessions** (W-49: phone visible, hands and pages only, a spoken "fine" on the file, the voice held as Tier C for release 4d). Slip protocol: stop, assess in ten minutes, write it down within the hour, tell the advisor at the next session, choose carry on / partial reveal / convert / stop, fix the cause, log it. Guests are invited to a gathering, not to a film (Section 11). The only-me question is re-opened once, at TD-L2c on Sun 3 Jan, never silently. **No temple or haveli office is ever asked anything** (asking is telling); no hum goes to a tune-identification vendor without the maker's OK (O-74). **Phone hygiene** (W-26, W-74): two project phones with no accounts, backup off, airplane mode, fixtures only, WhatsApp absent; nothing of the film reaches any phone before the reveal, including "send as document to myself"; her own phone is never looked at before the reveal; no QR code, no live link, no teaser on the night.
