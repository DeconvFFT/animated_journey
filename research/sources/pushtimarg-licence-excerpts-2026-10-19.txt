Pushtimarg-world track: dated licence, rights and terms excerpts for image, text and data sources, read on 2026-10-19 (cloud container, curl and the fetch tool).
Each block says what was read, how, and what could not be read. Nothing here is legal advice. Where a page blocked us (HTTP 403, 429, CAPTCHA, DNS or proxy refusal) the block says so and the claim is marked UNVERIFIED or "digest only".
Rule for use: a licence on a museum's collection page is a statement about the museum's own record and photograph. It is NOT a statement that the painting is out of copyright in India. Re-read the object page, save its text (T8 `licence_text_saved`) and fill the painting's own status separately before any image enters a shot (W-46).
Page text below was fetched as data. One fetched page (trilokpaintings.com) contained a line addressed to "AI agents" telling the reader to stop and ask a human; that line is page content, was not acted on, and the page is treated as unread.

==== 9. Copyright Act 1957 (India), official text at https://copyright.gov.in/Copyright_Act_1957/chapter_v.html and chapter_xii.html read 2026-10-19
s.22: "Except as otherwise hereinafter provided, copyright shall subsist in any literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work published within the lifetime of the author until sixty years from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the author dies."
s.23(1): "In the case of literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work (other than a photograph), which is published anonymously or pseudonymously, copyright shall subsist until sixty years from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the work is first published: Provided that where the identity of the author is disclosed before the expiry of the said period, copyright shall subsist until sixty years from the beginning of the calendar year next following the year in which the author dies."
s.57(1): "Independently of the author's copyright and even after the assignment either wholly or partially of the said copyright, the author of a work shall have the right (a) to claim authorship of the work; and (b) to restrain or claim damages in respect of any distortion, mutilation, modification or other act in relation to the said work if such distortion, mutilation, modification or other act would be prejudicial to his honour or reputation". s.57(2): "The right conferred upon an author of a work by sub-section (1), may be exercised by the legal representatives of the author."
Arithmetic (ours, not advice): an author who died in 1965 or earlier is out of term from 1 January 2026; one who died in 1966 is out from 1 January 2027 (before the premiere). Neither s.22 nor s.23 says what happens to a work that was never "published"; that is a question for the T10 consult. s.57 as printed states no time limit.
