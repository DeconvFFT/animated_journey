B. Smithsonian Open Access (API, key DEMO_KEY; https://api.si.edu/openaccess/api/v1.0/search?q=1945-54-8)
 - Hanging, Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum (CHNDM), object number 1945-54-8, 19th century, made in India, media usage "CC0". This is the Saurashtra toran in Kathipa style described by Cooper Hewitt (https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2016/07/11/a-traditional-toran/): "Women of the Kathi landlord caste were the first to practice embroidery in the Kathipa style"; "silk embroidered on a blue cotton foundation", mirrors inset around the borders; "Its eight hanging pennants are stylized mango leaves"; "symbols of love and fertility blessing the happy couple"; "Gift of Mary S. M. Gibson, 1945-54-8". The museum page itself states no licence; the CC0 flag is the API record's.
 - Bedcover (colcha), CHNDM 1955-103-3, 17th century, made in Gujarat, CC0: one of the Indo-Portuguese embroideries made for the Portuguese market (see the notes' exclusion list). The record text read does not itself say Christian imagery is present.
