Mobile-delivery track: dated text excerpts of the WhatsApp Help Center pages and one WhatsApp blog post, read on 2026-10-19 (about 05:15 to 05:25 UTC).

Why this file exists. The Help Center pages are script-rendered: the fetch tool returned only the page title for them, and a plain curl returned an HTTP 400 "Error" page. Fable's round-1 review of the mobile-delivery track (reviews/mobile-delivery-round1.md, item 1.7 and nudge 5) could not re-read the 64 MB, 100 MB and 2 GB figures, so this file records them.

How they were read. A headless Chromium (Playwright 1.56.1, user agent "HeadlessChrome/141.0.7390.37") ran in the cloud container, through the session's egress proxy, rendered each page, and the visible text (document.body.innerText) was copied. This is a real browser, but it is NOT the maker's own browser and it ran from a US-hosted container: WhatsApp may show other numbers by country or by app version, and the pages say "For users with a faster internet connection" without naming a country. Re-reading the four pages once in the maker's own browser is optional; if the numbers differ, the maker's reading wins and the playbook's sharing table (T-M4) is re-cut.
Page text is data. Nothing on these pages was acted on. Lines are verbatim; the page navigation and footer were left out. The "web" variant of the first page was read (cms_platform=web); the Android and iOS variants were not.

==== 1. WhatsApp Help Center, "How to send media, contacts, or location" (web variant). https://faq.whatsapp.com/453914586839706/?cms_platform=web. HTTP 200. Footer "© 2026 WhatsApp".
"Document to select documents, including PDFs, from your computer. Alternatively, you can drag and drop a document directly into the text field. The maximum allowed document size is 2 GB."
"To adjust the quality of an attached photo, click > Standard quality or HD quality > Done before clicking ."
"For users with a faster internet connection, the default video size limit is 100MB and 720p resolution."
"For users with a slower internet connection, the default video size limit is 64MB and 480p resolution."
"Status videos have a maximum duration of 90 seconds."
"HD media isn't currently available for status updates or profile pictures."
"For documents, the maximum file size allowed is 2 GB."
"High audio sample rate or professional cameras may be unsupported."
"The following video formats are supported: 3GP, AVI, FLV, MKV, MOV, MP4."

==== 2. WhatsApp Help Center, "Can't send longer videos" (the title today; an earlier reading of the same address called it "I can't send long videos"). https://faq.whatsapp.com/823886715479103/. HTTP 200.
"Users who have a slower internet connection might have difficulty sending large media files and HD media. The maximum file size for videos sent or forwarded through WhatsApp is 64MB on all platforms."
"For users with a faster internet connection, the default video size limit is 100MB and 720p resolution. For users with a slower internet connection, the default video size limit is 64MB and 480p resolution."
"However, if you'd like to send longer or larger media, you can also choose Document and select a photo or video. The maximum allowed document size is 2 GB."
"To send a video you've previously received, use the forward media feature in a chat to forward the video."
Reading note: the "conflict" the research note recorded between this page and page 1 is inside this one page: it states 64 MB "on all platforms" and, two lines later, 100 MB and 720p for a fast connection. The plan's "plan on 64 MB" stands. The page does not say that a document is left unchanged; it says only that Document is the way to send "longer or larger media".

==== 3. WhatsApp Help Center, "About forwarding limits". https://faq.whatsapp.com/1053543185312573. HTTP 200.
"You can forward messages to up to five people or groups at a time."
"If you're forwarding a message or update that was forwarded to you, it can only be shared with one additional group chat."
"If a message or update is forwarded through a chain of five or more chats it will: Be labeled as: "Forwarded many times.""
Reading note: the second line is new to the research note, which said only "five chats at a time". A copy the maker sends reaches up to five chats per forward; a relative who forwards it again is limited to one additional group chat by this page's text. Neither line stops forwarding, and a forwarded copy still cannot be recalled.

==== 4. WhatsApp Help Center, "About HD photos and videos" (Android variant). https://faq.whatsapp.com/759301289012856/?cms_platform=android. HTTP 200.
"Tap and select Standard quality or HD quality."
"If you attach an existing photo or video, you don't have to select between Standard quality or HD quality."
"To set HD quality as the default: Open WhatsApp Settings > Storage and data > Media upload quality. Select HD quality."
"The internet connection will impact the quality of media being sent and received, even if the media is sent in HD quality."
Reading note: the page gives no pixel or bitrate numbers for HD, as before. The new line says an existing video attached from the gallery offers no per-message choice; which quality it then gets is not stated.

==== 5. WhatsApp Blog, "Reactions, 2GB File Sharing, 512 Groups". https://blog.whatsapp.com/reactions-2gb-file-sharing-512-groups. HTTP 200. Posted May 5, 2022; "Last Updated: July 11, 2022".
"In addition, you can now send files within WhatsApp up to 2GB in size at a time, protected by end-to-end encryption. This is an increase from the previous limit of 100MB..."
"We recommend using WiFi for larger files and we'll display a counter while uploading or downloading to let you know how long your transfer will take."

What none of the five pages says (checked by reading them, not by searching): that a Document is delivered byte for byte; the resolution, bitrate or frame rate of the media route; how dark picture content is treated. Those remain UNVERIFIED until Exercise 8 and T-M6 row 9 run on two project phones with fixture content, after the maker decides Decision 12.
