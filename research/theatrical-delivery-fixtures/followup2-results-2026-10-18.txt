Follow-up round 2 results, 2026-10-18. Real DCP-o-matic 2.18.51 (official Ubuntu 24.04 command-line package, git 920fb9b044) run in a cloud container.
NOT the maker's Windows PC. Container: Ubuntu 24.04.5, 4 vCPU Intel Xeon 2.1 GHz (1 thread per core), 16 GB RAM, shared with other jobs
(load average 8.8 to 10.6 during the timing runs, so WALL times are inflated and unreliable; CPU seconds are the stable figure).
