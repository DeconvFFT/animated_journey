Then open ab_push_key.json. For each ladder clip it lists the injected position jitter (injected_rms_px) AND what mo8_audit.py measures on the decoded file (measured_centre_jit_px = MO8a).
MO8a reads about 1.3 to 1.6 times the injected position jitter (it is the frame-to-frame displacement residual, which adds a factor of about 1.33 for white jitter, plus the codec floor).
Take the lowest rung you marked 'yes' from the FRONT row and from the BACK row, and the lowest marked 'maybe'. Set the MO8a limit to half the measured_centre_jit_px of the lowest front-row 'yes',
